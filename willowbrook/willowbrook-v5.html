🌾 WILLOWBROOK

A COZY VILLAGE TALE

Click any villager to step into their shoes. Explore the village, chat with neighbors, harvest crops, fish at the pond — and watch the village live its day while you wander.

WASD or ←↑↓→ move  ·  Click select villager / walk there
E interact  ·  Tab switch villager  ·  Esc close

How to play

  • Click a villager — select & control them (ring + name appear).
  • WASD / arrows — walk. Click ground — walk there.
  • E or click glowing objects — talk, harvest, fish, sit, shop…
  • Tab — hop to the next villager. Uncontrolled villagers live their own day.
  • Earn 🪙: harvest crops, sell at the store, fish at the dock. Buy bread 🍞.
  • Villagers work by day, head home at night. Lamps light up after dark.
