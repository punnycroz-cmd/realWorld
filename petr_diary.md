# Petr's diary — the second spring

## Day 47 — arrival (dusk, rain)

Walked over the eastern pass at dusk. The valley opened below me — green, rain
coming down soft on the pines, the smell of wet bark. I have spent my life in
timber country and I know what it says: this is a place a man could work.

There is a hut by the stream. Roof sound, firepit cold, a bench, a timber pile,
a good table inside. Someone lived here before me. Their story is not mine to
know — but I am grateful, and I will pay it forward with interest.

Another figure on the far side — a woman, Sanna, a weaver's daughter, come up the
same day. A stranger. Perhaps a neighbor, in time.

PLAN: get to the hut before full dark, shelter the night. Tomorrow: food, footing,
work. Hunger is nothing yet (6 meals in the pack).
