# First 100 members — seeding plan

**Version:** v39 · 2026-09-24 · branch `sf/marketing` · LOCAL ONLY
**Parent spec:** `COMMUNITY-FUNNEL.md` §1 (funnel), §6 (creator outreach).
**Honesty constraint:** every member must arrive via real interest — no
invite farms, no paid joins, no "join for a chance at" mechanics.

The goal is not 100 members. The goal is a server where **a stranger who
joins on day-30 finds conversation worth reading.** That needs ~15–25
genuinely active watchers far more than it needs a big number.

---

## 1. Where the first members plausibly come from

| Source | Expected share | Channel ready? |
|---|---|---|
| Site visitors who finished how-it-works and want the next step | Largest | `community.html` CTA → invite (placeholder → live at go) |
| Wishlisters / devlog readers (itch.io devlog audience) | Second | devlog post templates in `templates/devlog-post.md` |
| Mission/SF locals & urban-sim niche (the real-neighborhood hook) | Meaningful minority | reddit-posts drafts (§3), local-interest angle |
| Life-sim community (Sims/Paralives/InZOI watchers between releases) | Meaningful minority | social drafts + creator outreach (funnel §6) |
| Friends of early members (organic) | Unknown, earned | referral-loop.md — never incentivized with gates |

Deliberately NOT sourced from: paid ads (none budgeted), Discord server
listing sites at launch (drive-by joins are churn), cross-promo with
unrelated communities.

## 2. Week-by-week shape

| Week | Target | What "done" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| W0 (launch) | 10–25 | Everyone who followed the pre-launch trail lands; owner greets each by name |
| W1 | 25–50 | First recap posts; `#the-feed` has member comments unprompted |
| W2–3 | 50–80 | First watch party runs (playbook); 1–2 members answer a newcomer before the owner does |
| W4 | ~100 | A quiet week still has conversation; day-30 retro decides if growth tactics change |

**If growth is faster:** cap nothing, but hold the moderation line — mods
recruited *after* day-14 per funnel §4.1 regardless of headcount.

**If growth is slower:** do NOT pivot to invite incentives. Fix the top of
the funnel (site→watch conversion) instead — a community can't be bribed
into existing, and 20 real watchers > 100 incentivized accounts.

## 3. The "first ten" job

The first ten members are the culture. Owner's explicit duties to them:

- Answer their questions in public channels so answers are searchable.
- Quote their best observations in the recap *with permission* — "a watcher
  noticed the baker rerouted around the fog" is better copy than anything
  we write, and it's real.
- Ask them directly what made them join — that answer is positioning data
  the market research can't give us.
- Never let them feel like testers. They're the audience.

## 4. Anti-vanity rules

- Never post member-count milestones. Celebrate *events* ("first community-
  filed exclusive request ran today"), never population.
- Never import the member list into anything else. No email harvesting,
  no cross-platform tracking.
- A member who only lurks and reads recaps is a Stage-2 success. The
  funnel's next rung is an offer, not a demand.

## 5. Retro checklist (day-30, feeds funnel §9)

- [ ] Did weekly recap cadence hold? (If not, fix cadence before growth.)
- [ ] What fraction of `#the-feed` discussion is member-initiated?
- [ ] Which source (§1) actually produced members? Double down honestly.
- [ ] Any joins traceable to creator coverage? → funnel §6 follow-up.
- [ ] Revisit: subreddit? forum? newsletter? (funnel §2 absent-list.)
