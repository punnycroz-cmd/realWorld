# Rent Week arc — 7-post narrative series keyed to the lease system

Channel: X (+Bluesky mirror) · Timing: T+7 → T+13 (post-launch week 2,
when the first in-world rent cycle has visible feed material) · Assets:
feed screenshots taken live that week + `site/shots/` stills
Gate: every "the feed will show" line below is pulled from the canonical
wording table (world/lease-ui.md §10 / leases.json). Post what's real —
if the week's actual feed is quiet, drop the post rather than invent
drama (feed-honesty rule, SOCIAL-LAUNCH-PLAN §7).

Why this arc: the rent cycle is the game's most legible systems story —
a thing every viewer already understands (rent is due) that demonstrates
memory, economy, and admin transparency without explaining the engine.

---

**Day 1 (Mon) — the setup**
> In Real World, rent runs on a calendar, not a vibe. Leases sign on the
> 1st, grace runs through the 5th, and the whole block can watch the
> parts that go public. This week: the block's first rent cycle. Thread
> below as it happens.

Attach: `site/shots/v31-B` still + "development build" label.

**Day 2 (Tue) — the money split**
> Two moneys, one block. Rent is game dollars — earned in-world, owed
> in-world. Credits are the viewer's currency: they buy agency (camera
> asks, event sponsorships, your own character's time), never rent.
> "Credits buy agency, not rent." It's on the tin.

Attach: pricing-card crop or the wire strip screenshot.

**Day 3 (Wed) — the ledger**
> Every tenant keeps a rent book: balance, grace window, itemized
> deductions, dispute filings. If a raise lands, the tenant can take it
> to the rent board — filing pauses the landlord's clock while the board
> reads. Evidence is the ledger, not anybody's word.

Attach: `site/shots/v16-int-flat` (interior still).

**Day 4 (Thu) — the public part**
> Late notices stay private. But if a tenancy goes far enough — cure-or-
> quit, an eviction filing, a rent-board ruling — it lands on the public
> feed with neutral wording. No amounts, no reasons. The block knows
> something happened; the ledger knows what.

Attach: live feed screenshot if a `housing`/`admin` event exists this
week — otherwise skip this post entirely.

**Day 5 (Fri) — the landlord layer**
> Owning a building here is earned, then bought: own a unit 30 days,
> keep standing and a clean record, then the landlord license is a deed
> fee on top. Owners can raise rent — inside caps, with notice ladders —
> and every admin action they take is feed-public. Power is visible.

Attach: `site/shots/v31-D` evening still.

**Day 6 (Sat) — the human question**
> People ask "can you just evict someone for fun?" Admin actions are
> attributed on the public feed — an owner who clears a building does it
> in front of everyone watching. And the mains can't be possessed or
> puppeted by anyone, owner included. Visibility is the leash.

Attach: none needed; text post.

**Day 7 (Sun) — the recap**

> is in the history browser if you want the receipts. Next Sunday: same
> time, whatever the block does next.
