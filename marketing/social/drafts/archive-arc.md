# Archive arc — 5-post series: "The block keeps receipts"

Channel: X (+Bluesky mirror) · Timing: T+14 → T+20 (post-launch week 3,
after the Archive has real days on it) · Assets: archive screenshots
taken live that week (see capture notes below) + `site/shots/` stills
Gate: **post-launch only.** The Archive reads live ledger data
(`gsWireDays`/`gsWireArchiveDay`, game-v6 contract); before that exists
every screenshot must carry the `demo archive` badge in-frame and the
post must say "demo". Never crop the badge out — the badge is the
honesty feature. Canon source: `world/archive-ui.md` (world-v20).

Why this arc: the Archive is the product's strongest trust signal —
every rumor is marked UNCONFIRMED, sourced to a place never a person,
and later resolved open / debunked / faded / stood. Every paid
intervention keeps its payer handle on the permanent record. "The block
keeps receipts" is the anti-creep answer we can *show* instead of argue.

---

**Post 1 (Mon) — the claim**
> Every game says "your actions matter." Ours keeps them on the record.
> The Archive is the block's permanent public ledger: every request a
> player paid for, every admin action, every rumor — attributed,
> timestamped, linkable. Free to read, forever.

Attach: Archive screenshot, ledger view. Caption must say
"demo archive" if shot pre-launch.

**Post 2 (Tue) — the rumor board**
> Word on the block travels like word on a block: distorted, secondhand,
> and marked. Rumor cards are badged UNCONFIRMED, later stamped: stood,
> debunked, faded, still open. {{N}} rumors on record, {{N}} debunked.
> The block settles its own stories.

Attach: rumor-board view screenshot. Fill {{N}} from the tally strip —
real numbers only; "0 debunked yet" is a fine post.

**Post 3 (Wed) — permalinks**
> See something happen on the wire? Link it. Every record in the Archive
> has a stable permalink — the same id it carried live. Quote-post the
> moment, not a screenshot of a screenshot. Receipts you can paste.

Attach: none needed; text post. Optional: a real `#e=` link if the
archive is live at post time — verify it resolves before posting.

**Post 4 (Thu) — who reached in**
> The ledger view answers the only question that matters in a game like
> this: who reached into the world? Every request event keeps its payer
> handle and its outcome — approved, modified, denied, refunded. The
> archive doesn't sanitize who paid. Neither do we.

Attach: ledger view screenshot. If a denied request is on record,
that's the best possible frame — "request not approved" on the permanent
record is the moderation system proving itself in public.

**Post 5 (Sun) — the honesty close**
> What the Archive won't show you: private whereabouts, a rumor's
> author, an hour nobody watched. Gaps say "off the feed (14:00 →
> 16:00)" instead of pretending. A history that admits its edges is
> worth more than one that doesn't.

Attach: a day view showing an honest-gap line, if one exists.
