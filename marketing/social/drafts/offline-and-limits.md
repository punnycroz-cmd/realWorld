# Limits arc — "the rules are the pitch" (5-post evergreen series)

Channel: X (+Bluesky mirror) · Timing: evergreen — banked for any quiet
week post-launch (first candidate slot T+28 onward, after Move-In Week)
· Assets: `site/shots/` stills, feed screenshots, none required for
text-only posts
Gate: every limit below is a real rule from the design doc
(`rw-game-design-2026-09-22.md`) or the shipped world contract — this
series only works because the limits are true. Do not soften them.

Why this arc: every other sim sells "do anything." Real World's
differentiator is what it refuses to do — no possessing the mains, no
anonymous requests, no cash-out, no frozen world when the AI coverage
thins. Saying the quiet rules out loud is both honesty and positioning:
the people who trust these limits are the audience.

---

**Post 1 — the possession ban**
> You can hire a character of your own and drive them — with a hard cap
> on session time. You cannot possess the eight mains. Not with credits,
> not ever. Their mornings belong to them. That's not a limitation we
> apologize for; it's the whole premise.

Attach: `site/shots/v48-A` still + "development build" label.

**Post 2 — the attributed feed**
> Every request in Real World is public and signed. Ask for rain over
> Dolores and the feed says who asked. Sponsor a block party and your
> handle is on the flyer. Nobody intervenes anonymously — the block keeps
> the receipts, and so does the Archive.

Attach: live feed screenshot if one exists; else a `site/shots/` still.

**Post 3 — no money out**
> Credits buy agency — camera asks, event sponsorships, your character's
> time. They never convert back to cash. There's nothing to earn, nothing
> to cash out, nothing to farm. The economy ends at the block's edge on
> purpose.

Attach: none needed; text post. (Cut-feature term is a denial — that's
the point of the post; the checker will WARN, accept it.)

**Post 4 — the Understudy**
> When AI coverage thins — an outage, a degraded lane — the block does
> not freeze. A fallback layer called the Understudy walks every resident
> through their real routine. All 28 routines cover the full 24 hours.
> The show degrades; it doesn't stop.

Attach: `site/shots/v48-C` still. Canon: world-v55 `thinai.json`
coverage ladders + main_routines C1–C8 (contiguous 0–24).

**Post 5 — the cap, again**
> Your hired character hands the wheel back to the AI at the session
> cap — mid-sentence if it has to. You can watch them finish the
> conversation without you. Some people find that eerie. We find it
> honest: the block was never yours.

Attach: none needed; text post. Good reply-bait in the best way — expect
"wait, really?" replies; `reply-bank.md` covers the possession-cap
questions.
