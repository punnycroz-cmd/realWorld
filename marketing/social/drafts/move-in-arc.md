# Move-In Week arc — 6-post series on hiring a character onto the cast

Channel: X (+Bluesky mirror) · Timing: T+21 → T+27 (post-launch week 4,
after rent-week and archive arcs — the funnel's last step gets its own
week once spectators understand the world) · Assets: `site/shots/`
stills + live feed screenshots of real hire/lease events if any exist
Gate: hire/creation wording is verbatim-safe from `world/creation.json`
+ `world/creation-ui.md` (world-v35 copy deck) and the design doc —
"hire a character onto the cast," never "create a character." The 500 cr
hire price is PROPOSAL-tier (monetization plan); confirm before send.
Post what's real — if no player has hired yet, the milestone posts say
so plainly (feed-honesty rule, SOCIAL-LAUNCH-PLAN §7).

Why this arc: every other arc sells watching. This one sells the last
funnel step — becoming a resident — and it only works because the rules
are honest: a hired character is the only kind you may ever possess,
a rented room is required, a human reviews the name, and the AI mind
keeps living when you log off.

---

**Post 1 (Mon) — the pitch**
> Watching is free forever. But if a block grows on you, there's a door:
> hire a character onto the cast. You pick a name (a human reads it),
> they need a room and a job like anyone else, and they're the only
> character you'll ever possess. {{URL}}/how-it-works.html

Attach: `site/shots/v48-A` still + "development build" label.

**Post 2 (Tue) — the math**
> Moving in costs credits and game dollars — the split is the point.
> The hire is 500 cr (provisional): the viewer's currency buying a
> capped cast slot. Then the block takes over — your character rents a
> room and pays a deposit in game dollars, earned in-world. Credits buy
> agency, not rent.

Attach: pricing-card crop or the wire strip screenshot.

**Post 3 (Wed) — the job**
> A hired character isn't a dress-up doll — they need work. The job
> board has real openings with real wages (the same board the AI
> residents use), and payroll runs Fridays. Pick badly and Friday is
> thin. The block does not grade on a curve.

Attach: `site/shots/v16-int-flat` interior still.

**Post 4 (Thu) — the leash**
> Your hired character is the only one you may ever possess — the eight
> mains are unpossessable by anyone. Log off and yours doesn't freeze:
> it drops to quiet lightweight AI and keeps its routines. Log back in
> and the full mind wakes up. You're a resident, not a puppeteer.

Attach: none needed; text post.

**Post 5 (Fri) — payday**
> Friday on the block is payday. If you hired in this week, your
> character's first envelope lands today — game dollars, itemized, real
> deductions. Watching someone else's payroll is spectator stuff;
> watching your own is different. {{URL}}/how-it-works.html

Attach: live feed screenshot if a `payroll`/`job` event ran — otherwise
a `site/shots/v48-D` evening still.
