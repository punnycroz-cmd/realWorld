# Seeded discussion starters — Discord / Reddit (post-launch)

Rules (SOCIAL-LAUNCH-PLAN §7): posted by the dev account, clearly as the
dev. Never sockpuppeted. Drop one every few days, only where conversation
isn't already happening on its own — seeding fills silence, it doesn't
steer buzz.

1. "Which resident have you found yourself watching longest? For us it
   keeps being the stoop — Carmen sees everything."
2. "The public request feed shows every intervention. Would you rather
   the feed also showed *denied* requests?"
3. "If you hired a character tomorrow, what's the first address you'd
   try to rent for them?"
4. "Weather requests are the cheapest thing on the menu. What would you
   actually spend a dollar on first?"
5. "The eight mains can't be possessed — by anyone, including us. Right
   call, or would you want a shot at driving a main?"
6. "Someone on the block is writing about all of them. Your theories —
   go." (Let it run; never confirm.)
7. "What should 'This Week on the Block' cover that we're missing?"
8. "Ambience check: what's the best time of day to leave the block
   running on a second monitor?"
9. "If the block expanded by one street, which direction should it grow?"
10. "Show us your favorite screenshot from the feed this week."
