# DRAFT → PUBLISHED — Devlog 19: "A new face can arrive wanting something."

Status: PUBLISHED on `site/journal.html` (v192, dated 2026-09-24),
text-only per the template. Accuracy checklist verified against
world-v133 sources (`world/creation-ui.md` v133 spec section,
`world/creation.json` v29 `record_schema.hope` / `hope_layer_v133` /
`time_budget_v133`, `world/create.html` v10, shared-inbox world-v133
entry):

- **What they carried in** — step 5 of character creation gains an
  optional single pick off the `HOPES` mirror: a trade to learn, people
  to eat with, a crew, to be good at the work, a place that's theirs
  someday, to be known a little, a quiet year — plus the honest default
  "arrives open". Structured pick (data, not free text), so it adds
  zero screening surface.
- **A hope is not a promise** — the build's own copy, quoted verbatim:
  an initial condition the brain may keep, revise, or quietly drop, and
  nothing tracks the difference. No quest log, progress meter, check-in,
  or score exists anywhere. A hope naming a face (Tomás's patience,
  Carmen's table, Marcus's circle, Sam's pitch) names them *without
  asking* — nobody was asked; an abandoned hope is a legitimate outcome,
  not a failed quest.
- **Grounded like the open doors** — every HOPES entry carries a reach
  channel the audit re-verifies: `at` a job-board employer, `who` a face
  on the PEOPLE mirror, `near` a 9xxx prefix on the card, `job` the
  picker's own post, `open` needs nothing. The hopes can't drift from
  the block.
- **The week's cost (HOURS row)** — the first-week grid prints the
  picked job's posted hours where the commitment is made: a 40-hour
  card eats evenings ("a hope competes with sleep"), a 30-hour card
  leaves thin evenings, a side shift leaves the week open but the money
  thin, `variable` reads "the catch is the pay, not the clock", and
  arriving without work owns every hour while watching every dollar.
- **Where it rides** — `params.hope` on the bus filing: recorded,
  unread, same posture as `goes_by` (flagged for the game track). On the
  step-6 quote ("Carried in"), the day-one keys card, and the registry
  entry ("initial condition, never a tracked goal"). **Never a briefing
  line** — the briefing whitelist stays public profile / surface
  relationships / routine; hope is never metered.
- Direction fit (production-3): self-chosen projects as initial
  conditions, never assigned destinies; limited capacity stated at the
  point of commitment. This is the in-world counterpart of the observer
  loop's "choose someone to follow."
