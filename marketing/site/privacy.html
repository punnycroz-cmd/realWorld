Privacy, in plain language.

A game about watching people should be honest about who's watching you. The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no third-party trackers, and never sees your card number. What little we do collect is listed below — all of it, not the polite parts.

On this website

What the site collects.

Anonymous page analytics

When analytics are enabled, a first-party script records page views and button clicks: page path, page title, referring host (never the full URL), language, and campaign parameters on landing. No cookies, no localStorage identifiers, no fingerprinting. Your "session" is a random nonce that dies when the tab closes.

Unique-visitor counting happens on our own server with a daily-rotating IP+UA+salt hash — yesterday's count can't be tied to today's, and the client never sends a persistent ID. The backend is self-hosted; nothing goes to an ad network.

What the site never does

  • No tracking pixels, no social embeds, no ad-tech scripts.
  • No accounts on the marketing site — there is nothing to log in to.
  • No cross-tab or cross-day visitor identity.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data. There's barely any to sell.

We honor Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control, and ?nocollect=1. You can also opt out permanently from the browser — set localStorage "rw:no-collect" to "1", or just don't visit; we won't know either way.

In the game

What playing creates.

Your handle & asks

Spectating needs no account. If you reach in, you pick a public handle — screened so it can't be a cast member's or a real person's name — and your requests carry it on the public feed, permanently. Assume everything you file is public, because it is.

The ledger

Credits spent, refunds issued, and moderation decisions are recorded in a ledger so the money side stays auditable. Public feed entries show your handle and what ran — never your payment details.

What we don't store

Card numbers, bank details, billing addresses beyond what Stripe requires for tax — checkout is hosted by Stripe and card data never touches our servers. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to what it processes.

The fine print

Age, retention, and your rights.

Children

Watching is open to everyone — it's a public feed, no account. Under-13 visitors are directed to a spectator-only experience and out of every paid feature: no purchases, no requests, no rewarded ads. Players under 18 get limits on spend-related features; rewarded ad views are adult-only.

Retention & deletion

Site analytics: aggregate counts persist; the per-visitor hash rotates daily. Game-side: your handle, request history, and ledger entries are kept while the world runs — the public feed is the game's memory, and your attributed asks are part of its record. To ask about access or deletion of account-level data, write to the contact below.

Changes

If this policy changes materially — a new collection category, a new processor — it gets a dated entry on this page and a note in the launch journal before it takes effect. The privacy posture (cookieless, self-hosted, no third parties) is a product commitment, not a loophole to close later.

Contact

Questions, access requests, or anything about this page: privacy@realworld-game.example (placeholder address — activates when the domain is registered; see the press kit for press contact).
