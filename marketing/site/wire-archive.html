Reading the archive

Word on the block entries are talk overheard in public — always UNCONFIRMED, sourced to a place, never a person. When the talk resolves, the archive shows how it landed.

Gaps are honest. Hours with no public events stay empty; the archive doesn't fill dead air. Today holds through the last completed hour.

Attribution is verbatim. The ledger shows who paid for what, exactly as the live wire showed it — admin actions included.

What the archive is: the same spectator layer the live wire shows — arrivals, crowds, weather, attributed requests, word on the block. Nothing was added in hindsight and nothing was cut. Homes are walls — "headed home" is where coverage ends. A rumor can only ever be a distorted retelling of something already public; denied request text was never written anywhere, so it can't be found here either. Event ids are stable: a permalink that worked on the wire resolves here tomorrow, unchanged.
