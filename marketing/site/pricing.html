Pricing

Free to watch. Priced to reach in.

Observation costs nothing, forever. Credits — the meta currency — buy moments of agency and a place in the cast. Every price is printed before you pay; nothing is randomized.

Provisional
Every number on this page is a pre-launch planning figure anchored to published competitor pricing — not a live store price. Final pricing is set at launch and this page will say so when it is.
Final pricing. These are the launch prices. If they ever change, this page changes first — and any purchase made before a change keeps the terms it was bought on.

Credit packs

Credits are spent on requests, world changes, and character creation. They're non-transferable, never redeemable for money, and never expire. About a cent each — the same unit price players already know from Robux and V-Bucks.

  • Regional pricing: packs are priced in local currency on a published, purchasing-power-adjusted table. No silent geo-markup — and since credits can't be transferred, regional pricing can't be arbitraged.

Or earn them. Opt-in rewarded ads pay 2 credits per view, capped at 5 per day — about $0.45 a week of free credits. Enough for a small request, deliberately never a wage. Credits can't be farmed by gameplay grinding; that's the wall that keeps gray markets out.

What requests cost

Every request declares its action and exact duration upfront and pays in credits scaled to that duration. Hard cap — no overruns, no debt. When time ends, control hands back gracefully to the AI.

Request classRate (provisional)≈ USDSession cap

Exclusive — locks a shared resource (a venue, an event window)6 cr/min~$3.30/hr60 min
Queued — fires when the slot freesclass rate −15%—same
  • 15-minute minimum — a 15-min compatible session is ~22 credits, about twenty cents. The cheapest moment of agency costs less than a gumball.
  • Surge pricing is honest: ×1.5–2.5 on a resource that was exclusively used in the last 6 hours or during prime-time windows — shown on the request form before you pay. Never after.
  • Queued requests that expire before activation are auto-refunded. Admin overrides that cancel your run are refunded in credits.
  • Cooldowns are never for sale. They're a fairness mechanism, not a revenue lever — you can't pay to skip them.
  • Every request posts to the public feed — you always know what a moment cost and who bought it.

Subscriptions (provisional)

Optional, monthly, cancelable. Perks are discounted currency, convenience, and cosmetics — never power over free players.

Resident — $4.99/mo

  • 600 credits/mo stipend (≈$5.40 bought à la carte)
  • +1 character slot (total 2)
  • Queued-request priority — tie-breaks in your favor within the queued class only; it can never preempt an active session
  • Weekly illustrated life digest for your street
  • Profile badge; no daily ad grind needed for the stipend

Director — $11.99/mo

  • Everything in Resident
  • 1,500 credits/mo total stipend
  • +1 more character slot (total 3)
  • Camera director mode unlocked — multi-cam, replays, clips
  • Monthly exclusive cosmetic; name in the show credits
  • Early access to new request types

Watching stays free without either tier — always. The fairness audit: every perk is (a) discounted currency, (b) convenience or cosmetics, or (c) a view-layer feature. None touches what a free player can do in the world.

Joining the cast

Character slot — 500 credits provisional

One-time per character (≈$4.50). Includes basic wardrobe and the starter apartment hunt. Max 3 characters per account. Creating a character also requires renting in-game housing with game dollars — nobody skips the lease.

Housing — game dollars only

Rooms from ~$700/mo to whole Victorians at ~$5,500/mo — all in in-world currency earned by working a job ($16–50/hr). Credits cannot pay rent. Miss rent and the admin landlord can evict, on the public record. That's what makes owning mean something.

Cosmetics — direct purchase only

Outfits 150–400 cr, premium sets 600–900 cr, furniture 100–1,200 cr, vanity touches (renames, unit plaques) 100–250 cr — all provisional. Everything is previewed before purchase. There is no chance mechanic anywhere in the store.

What money can never do here

Never buys the cast

The eight mains can't be possessed at any price — by anyone, including us.

Never cashes out

Credits are a closed loop: non-transferable, non-redeemable, no trading, no exchange for game dollars in either direction.

Never hides its odds

There are none — no loot boxes, no gacha. Every price is printed before you pay, surge included.
