Pricing

Free to watch. Priced to reach in.

Observation costs nothing, forever. Credits — the meta currency — buy moments of agency and a place in the cast. Here's the whole shape of it.

Provisional
Every number on this page is a pre-launch planning figure anchored to published competitor pricing — not a live store price. Final pricing is set at launch and this page will say so when it is.

Credit packs

Credits are spent on requests, world changes, and character creation. They're non-transferable and never redeemable for money.

Or earn them. Opt-in rewarded ads pay a few credits per view, capped per day — enough to file a small compatible request without ever opening a wallet. Credits can never be farmed by gameplay grinding; that's deliberate, and it keeps gray markets out.

What requests cost

Every request declares its action and duration upfront and is priced in credits scaled to that duration. Hard cap — no overruns.

Request classExampleCost (provisional)
Compatible — runs alongside other activityAn open-mic night at the café for 30 min25–75 credits
Exclusive — locks a character or resourcePossess your own character for 1 hour; call rain for 2 hours150–400 credits + peak-hour surge
Queued — fires when the slot freesSame as above, on a waitlistSame class price, ~10% less
  • Queued requests that expire before activation are auto-refunded.
  • Admin overrides that cancel your run are refunded in credits.
  • Every request posts to the public feed — you always know what a moment cost and who bought it.

Joining the cast

Character creation slot provisional

500 credits per character, or included with a Resident subscription. Creating a character also requires renting in-game housing with game dollars — nobody skips the lease.

Resident subscription provisional

$4.99/mo: a monthly credit stipend (600), one extra character slot, priority position in the request queue, and a profile badge. Watching stays free without it — always.

The anti-pay-to-win rule, in one sentence: status is earned in-world and only accelerated by spend. Dollars can't buy landlord standing, and credits can't buy reputation.

What money can never do here

Never buys the cast

The eight mains can't be possessed at any price — by anyone, including us.

Never cashes out

Credits are a closed loop: non-transferable, non-redeemable, no trading, no gifting of exclusive requests.

Never hides its odds

There are none — no loot boxes, no gacha. Every price is printed before you pay.
