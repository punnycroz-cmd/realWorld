  • 15-minute minimum — a 15-min compatible session is ~22 credits, about twenty cents. The cheapest moment of agency costs less than a gumball.
  • Surge pricing is honest: ×1.5–2.5 on a resource that was exclusively used in the last 6 hours or during prime-time windows — shown on the request form before you pay. Never after.
  • Queued requests that expire before activation are auto-refunded. Admin overrides that cancel your run are refunded in credits.
  • Cooldowns are never for sale. They're a fairness mechanism, not a revenue lever — you can't pay to skip them.
  • Every request posts to the public feed — you always know what a moment cost and who bought it.

Subscriptions (provisional)

Optional, monthly, cancelable. Perks are discounted currency, convenience, and cosmetics — never power over free players.

Resident — $4.99/mo

  • 600 credits/mo stipend (≈$5.40 bought à la carte)
  • +1 character slot (total 2)
  • Queued-request priority — tie-breaks in your favor within the queued class only; it can never preempt an active session
  • Weekly illustrated life digest for your street
  • Profile badge; no daily ad grind needed for the stipend

Director — $11.99/mo

  • Everything in Resident
  • 1,500 credits/mo total stipend
  • +1 more character slot (total 3)
  • Camera director mode unlocked — multi-cam, replays, clips
  • Monthly exclusive cosmetic; name in the show credits
  • Early access to new request types

Watching stays free without either tier — always. The fairness audit: every perk is (a) discounted currency, (b) convenience or cosmetics, or (c) a view-layer feature. None touches what a free player can do in the world.

Joining the cast

Character slot — 500 credits provisional

One-time per character (≈$4.50). Includes basic wardrobe and the starter apartment hunt. Max 3 characters per account. Creating a character also requires renting in-game housing with game dollars — nobody skips the lease.

Housing — game dollars only

Rooms from ~$700/mo to whole Victorians at ~$5,500/mo — all in in-world currency earned by working a job ($16–50/hr). Credits cannot pay rent. Miss rent and the admin landlord can evict, on the public record. That's what makes owning mean something.

Regional pricing — the method, published

A dollar isn't the same price everywhere. Packs are sold in local currency on a purchasing-power-adjusted table — and the whole method is public, because an honest price list that changes at the border isn't honest.

One credit ladder

Same packs, same credit amounts, everywhere. What changes is the local-currency price of a pack — never what a credit buys inside the world.

PPP-adjusted, published

Local prices follow a purchasing-power index (Steam-style regional pricing). The full table publishes at launch — before that, treat every local figure as provisional like the rest of this page.

No arbitrage by design

Credits are non-transferable and non-redeemable, so a cheaper region can't be farmed for resale. EU/UK prices display tax-inclusive, as consumer law requires.

Payments run through Stripe with Stripe Tax at launch; a merchant-of-record (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy) takes over when EU/UK volume justifies it. Nothing here is a surcharge — only the local equivalent of the same printed ladder.

If prices ever change — the promise

Quick answers

Pricing-specific, in one place. The full FAQ covers the rest — possession, moderation, the two currencies.

Do credits expire?

No — no expiration, no dormancy fees. Credits only ever move three ways: you spend them, a refund returns them, or they sit there.

What can I buy for under a dollar?

The Pocket pack — 100 credits for $0.99 — covers about four 15-minute compatible sessions. The cheapest moment of agency costs less than a gumball.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — expired queue slots, denied reviews, early admin overrides, and declined nudges all refund automatically in credits. The table in the refund ledger lists every case.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Watching is free forever and every paid action works à la carte. Resident and Director are optional — their perks are discounted currency, convenience, and cosmetics, never power.

Can kids buy credits?

Minor accounts are hard-capped below the default $200/day spend cap, and rewarded-ad credits are adults-only and opt-in only.

Is a subscription worth it?

Only if your month uses more credits than the Starter pack holds. The breakeven widget prices your exact pace three ways and tells you — including when the answer is "don't subscribe".

Can I gift credits to a friend?

No — credits are non-transferable by design. It's the same rule that blocks trading, cash-out, and every gray market around them.

What money can never do here

Never buys the cast

The eight mains can't be possessed at any price — by anyone, including us.

Never cashes out

Credits are a closed loop: non-transferable, non-redeemable, no trading, no exchange for game dollars in either direction.

Never hides its odds

There are none — no loot boxes, no gacha. Every price is printed before you pay, surge included.
