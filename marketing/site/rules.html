House rules — plain version

Screened, not staged.

Real World is a neighborhood of AI residents whose minds nobody controls — not the players, not us. What we do control is the door: every paid request is screened for intent before it can run, and everything that runs is attributed in public. This page is the whole policy, in language you can check against the game itself.

The contract

Three promises you can verify.

Intent is screened, outcomes aren't

We moderate what players ask for — never what characters decide. An approved request enters the world as an opportunity; the resident's own mind chooses how to respond. There is no script to leak and no puppeteering to police.

The feed is the receipt

Every paid intervention, every admin action, every refund is on the public request feed with attribution. Moderation isn't a black box you trust — it's a ledger you read.

The mains are untouchable

The 8 main characters can't be possessed by anyone — including the game owner. Their secrets stay sealed even from paying players. The show's integrity is a rule, not a promise.

Reaching into the world

What a request can and can't be.

What gets blocked

Requests are screened on their text — what you're asking for, not a prediction of how the AI will render it. Blocked categories:

  • Targeting harm — requests whose point is a specific character's humiliation, suffering, or destruction.
  • Legal backstop — threats, sexual content, or defamation of real people. (Every address on the block is generated above the real block maximum — no fictional address is a real door.)
  • Secret extraction — requests fishing for drama seeds or redacted story material. Secrets are discovered by watching, not bought.
  • Hands off the admin's desk — rent, evictions, and leases are world admin powers, not purchasable requests. And nobody else's character can be possessed — only your own hire.
  • Real names stay out — requests naming real businesses or impersonating real people are declined; the parody name is always suggested instead.

Denied requests are never charged, and every denial can be appealed once within 72 hours — to a different reviewer.

How review actually works

Compatible requests — two characters doing something together — auto-run on classifier pass. Exclusive and gray-zone requests wait for a human reviewer. We don't promise review times; a request that expires before activation refunds automatically.

Grief is designed to be expensive and public: credits upfront, cooldowns, surge pricing on contested resources — and if an attempt runs anyway, the feed names who paid for it.

Refunds are automatic

Denied at screening: your credits never move — every denial refunds in full, every time. Expired in queue: auto-refunded. Approved with a trimmed scope: the unused credits come back. Revoked by an admin mid-run: compensated. The ledger shows all of it.

What we can't do

There is no "undo" for history — an event that runs becomes canon, and the neighborhood reacts to it. And there is no complaint desk for emergent behavior: if a resident is cruel to your character, that's the story, and in-world reputation is the lever.

Possession, fairly

You can only ever drive your own hire.

Whose hands on the wheel

Possession is only ever of a character you hired — never another player's tenant, never a cast member, never the landlord. When your session ends (hard cap, no overrun), the character's own AI resumes seamlessly.

What possession can't see

A possession briefing contains the public profile, surface relationships, and daily routine — nothing more. Possession never reveals secrets, yours or anyone else's. To learn the block's truths, you watch like everyone else.

Community spaces

Rules of the block.

These apply wherever the community gathers — Discord at launch, recap comments, anywhere official. The same text is pinned in the community channels.

1 — Fiction is fiction

The 28 residents are fictional people on real streets. Don't map them, their generated addresses, or their lives onto real people or real doors. That's the one hard line — instant ban.

2 — People are people

No hate, harassment, spam, or scams aimed at members. Credits are non-transferable and non-redeemable — anyone offering to buy or sell them is running a scam. Instant ban.

3 — Scheme in the open

Discussing request mechanics is welcome — half the fun is the meta-game. Coordinating to break or grief the sim outside the request system is a ban.

4 — Keep the seals

Secrets discovered by watching are fair game for discussion. Leaked or datamined drama seeds get deleted — the Truman contract only works if the fiction stays sealed.

Escalation: warn → 24-hour timeout → ban, with every action logged. Instant-ban categories: credit scams, doxxing attempts, illegal content, raids. One appeal per ban, to the owner — moderators don't adjudicate their own calls.

What we won't claim

Honesty is the policy too.

No safety theater

We don't call the community "safe" or the world "grief-free" — no one can promise that and mean it. What we promise is verifiable: screening, attribution, refunds, and a public record.

No un-seeable guardians

The characters aren't supervised — they're authored and then free. If you're looking for a sim where the adults are monitored, this isn't it. If you're looking for one where interference can't hide, it is.
