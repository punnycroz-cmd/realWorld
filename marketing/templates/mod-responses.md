# Moderator canned responses — Real World ("The Mission")

Fill-in templates for the two moderation surfaces (MODERATION-PLAN.md is the
authority). Brackets = fill before posting. Tone: plain, kind, no corporate
wallpaper. Mods use these verbatim or lightly adapted — mods never improvise
*policy*, only phrasing. Owner posts anything that announces or changes rules.

## A. Request-pipeline responses (posted by the system / owner, attributed on feed)

**Denied — intent screening (auto or review):**
> Request [#ID] denied under our request rules (reason: [harm-targeting /
> legal-backstop / secret-extraction]). Your credits were never charged — the
> hold released automatically. You're welcome to refile a different request.

**Denied — repeat abuse (no refund, second+ offense):**
> Request [#ID] denied. Because this repeats a previously denied category, the
> filing charge stands per the request rules. Details: [one line].

**Expired in queue (auto-refund):**
> Request [#ID] expired before activation — a human never reached it in time.
> Full refund issued. The queue is first-come-first-served; compatible
> requests are the fast lane.

**Admin override of an active request:**
> Admin action: request [#ID] was revoked by the owner. Affected players were
> compensated [X] credits. This action is public like every intervention.

## B. Community-space responses (Discord / comments)

**Warn:**
> Hey [name] — that crosses house rule [#N] ([short rule name]). First and
> only warning; details in #rules. Happy to explain in DM.

**Timeout:**
> [name] timed out 24h — repeat of rule [#N]. Logged in #mod-log. Appeal path
> is in the rules channel if you think we got it wrong.

**Ban:**
> [name] banned — [rule / instant-ban category]. Logged. Appeals go to the
> owner via the press-kit contact address; one appeal per ban.

**Credit-scam PSA (after a scam post is removed):**
> Reminder: credits are non-transferable and can never be sold or traded.
> Anyone offering them is scamming — report and don't engage.

**Doxxing/fiction-mapping removal:**
> Removed — we don't map fictional characters or generated addresses onto
> real people or real doors. That's the one hard line here; it's an instant
> ban. (Why: every address on the block is generated to be impossible as a
> real household's.)

**"The AI did something awful to my character" (not a moderation case):**
> That's the world, not a rule violation — characters run their own minds and
> nobody can direct their dialogue, including us. In-world reputation is the
> lever; your character can respond in kind through play. If a *request*
> targeted you, that's different — link the feed entry and we'll look.

**"Is X allowed as a request?" (pre-filing question):**
> Short answer: if the request text targets a specific character's harm or
> humiliation, it'll be screened and refunded — don't spend the filing.
> Compatible requests (two characters doing something together) auto-run and
> are the cheapest, fastest way in.

## C. Never-use list

- Never say "the devs will look into the character's behavior" — emergent
  behavior is not actionable.
- Never promise review times ("should be looked at today").
- Never discuss another member's moderation history publicly — actions are
  logged, not narrated.
- Never confirm or deny whether a character's secret exists — seeds stay
  sealed even when players guess right.
