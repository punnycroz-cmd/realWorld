## 3. Embargo policy

- **Default:** no embargo for the kit itself — it's public-equivalent
  material once the site is live.
- **Pre-announcement outreach (if used):** offer embargoed preview access to
  2–3 priority outlets, with a single embargo datetime stated in the pitch
  ("embargoed until [DATE] [TIME] PT"). Get explicit agreement *before*
  sending material beyond the public kit.
- **Embargo kit contents:** fact sheet, screenshots, key art, founder quote
  (when written). Never embargo-share internal economics or the research
  report.
- **Breach handling:** if an outlet breaks embargo, note it in the outreach
  table and deprioritize them for exclusives; no retaliation beyond that.
- **No paid coverage, no review keys-for-coverage deals.** If an outlet asks
  for compensation or in-game perks for coverage, decline.

## 4. Cadence and follow-up

- One follow-up max, ≥4 business days later, in the same thread.
- Log every contact in the outlet table (SENT / REPLIED / PUBLISHED / DECLINED).
- Creator/streamer outreach is a separate lane — see SOCIAL-LAUNCH-PLAN when
  that doc lands; don't cross-pitch press and creators with the same email.
