## Words to avoid (and the accurate version)

- ~~"control the characters"~~ → you control **your own** character; the
  eight mains are unpossessable by design.
- ~~"command the world"~~ → requests are **screened opportunities**, not
  orders. Conflicting asks queue; denied asks refund.
- ~~"playable now" / "out now"~~ → in development; no public build yet.
- ~~"an AI-generated reality show"~~ → the world is authored (written cast,
  written rules); the AI performs it. Don't imply content is unreviewed.
- ~~"NPCs"~~ → "residents" or "characters." (Fine to say NPC once for SEO
  flavor in your own voice — just not as our label for the mains.)
- ~~"earn money in-game"~~ → there is no cash-out. Game dollars pay rent
  and wages inside the world; credits only buy agency.
- ~~"voice-acted"~~ → no voice acting or TTS. The feed is text.
- ~~"crypto / tokens / NFT"~~ → no. Flat no, don't even ask it as a
  headline hook without the answer attached: no tokens, no cash-out.
- ~~"they think they're human"~~ → inverted. The mains know they're AI;
  what they don't know is that it's a simulation — or that you're watching.
- ~~"sentient / conscious / self-aware AI"~~ → don't claim minds we can't
  verify. Say "developing": they form intentions, change over weeks,
  surprise us.
- ~~"just like real people"~~ → never mimicry claims. The show is becoming,
  not imitation.

## Pricing phrasing (until launch numbers are final)

- Safe: "credits priced roughly a cent each, in packs from under a dollar"
  and "a camera-director request runs about a dime for thirty minutes."
- All figures are **provisional** — say so. Final prices are set at launch.
- Never quote a subscription price without the word "optional" next to it.
  Watching never costs anything and the subscription never gates watching.
