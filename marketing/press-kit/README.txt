Usage rules for press/creators
------------------------------
  - Editorial use with attribution: "Real World (in development)".
  - Screenshots and key art show a development build; keep the
    "in development" label when publishing them.
  - Logo: on dark backgrounds use logo-primary; on light/white use
    logo-primary-dark. Do not recolor,
    stretch, or redraw the lit-window mark. Do not imply endorsement.
  - Do not present placeholder contact fields as real contact info.
  - Describing the mechanics? copy-deck.md has verbatim-safe phrasing and
    a words-to-avoid list. Creators: read creator-notes.md before covering.

Accuracy note
-------------
  Everything in this kit reflects the locked design: watching is free;
  paid agency is time-boxed requests with hard caps; the 8 main
  characters can never be possessed by anyone; no voice acting, no
  loot boxes, no cash-out, no crypto. If a fact seems missing, it's
  probably undecided — ask rather than assume.
