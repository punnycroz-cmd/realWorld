# Press Q&A — anticipated questions, approved answers

> Internal aid for interviews and email replies. Every answer is accurate to
> the locked design — keep them that way. If a question isn't covered here,
> answer "not decided yet" rather than improvising a feature.

**Is this a game or a show?**
Both, deliberately. The free layer is a show — a neighborhood that runs 24/7
whether you watch or not. The paid layer turns viewers into participants,
but only through time-boxed requests, never through buying the cast.

**Who plays the main characters?**
Nobody. The eight mains run on full AI minds around the clock, and the
possession ban means no human — including the game's owner — can take them
over. That's the audience contract: the drama is real because nobody can
steer it.

**So what do players actually control?**
Their own characters, if they hire one — and time-boxed requests into the
world (possess *your* character, change the weather, trigger an event).
Every request declares its action and duration upfront, pays upfront, caps
hard, and posts to a public feed.

**Can a player buy their way into ruining the world?**
No. Conflicting requests are auto-classified (exclusive/compatible/queued),
cooldowns and surge pricing throttle world-scale actions, the owner holds a
deny switch with automatic refunds, and reputation consequences persist on
characters. And the mains can't be touched at all.

**Is the neighborhood real?**
The geography is — real Mission streets, real Dolores Park. The people and
addresses are fictional: house numbers are generated above real block
maximums so they can never map to a real door, and businesses use parody
names.

**How much does it cost?**
Watching: nothing. Requests: credits, priced per minute of agency — think
coffee-priced, not DLC-priced (figures provisional until launch). Optional
subscription, character slots. Credits are non-transferable; there's no
cash-out, no loot boxes, no crypto.

**Can I possess a character and read its secrets?**
No — possession never reveals secrets. A possession briefing contains the
public profile, surface relationships, and routine. Drama seeds are redacted
even from the person driving.

**What happens when I stop possessing my character?**
The AI resumes seamlessly. An offline player-owned character drops to cheap
"thin" AI and wakes to a full brain when its player returns.

**Can my character skip rent?**
It can try. In-world rent, wages, and groceries run on game dollars; miss
rent and the landlord/admin tools handle it like they would for anyone —
up to eviction.

**Is this an NFT/crypto thing?**
No. No tokens, no cash-out, no RMT. Credits only ever buy agency inside the
world.

**Who's it for?**
People who watch streams more than they play; sim and management players
(RimWorld, Dwarf Fortress, Sims); Mission locals who want to argue about
whether we got the block right.
