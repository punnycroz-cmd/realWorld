# Coverage log — Real World ("The Mission")

**Status:** EMPTY TEMPLATE — nothing has been published or covered yet.
This ledger starts filling the day coverage begins. It is the kit's
institutional memory: who covered us, what they used, what they got
right, and what we asked them to fix.

Keep one row per piece of coverage. The point is not a vanity clip
file — it is the accuracy loop. Every row ends in either "clean" or a
tracked correction, because our whole pitch is that the product is
exactly as described.

## How to fill a row

1. **Log it the day you see it.** A reader finding a week-old error is
   a week of readers believing it.
2. **Check claims, not tone.** A negative review that describes the
   product accurately is a *clean* row. A glowing piece that says
   "possess any resident" is a correction.
3. **Corrections go through the deadline-desk policy** — friendly
   note, the exact sentence, the accurate replacement, a linkable
   source. One correction per outlet per piece; don't relitigate.
4. **Copy-deck is the ruler.** A claim is "wrong" only if it
   contradicts `copy-deck.md`, the fact sheet, or the shipped product —
   not because we dislike the wording.

## Claims most likely to be misstated (check every row for these)

- "You can possess the characters" — **wrong.** The 8 mains can never
  be possessed by anyone; paid agency is a time-boxed *request* an
  AI resident can refuse.
- "Free demo" / "free trial" — **wrong framing.** Watching is the
  free tier and it is the product's core, not a preview.
- "Pay to win / pay for story" — requests are opportunities, never
  guaranteed outcomes; the feed shows them with attribution.
- "Crypto / NFTs / cash-out" — **wrong.** Credits are spend-only;
  nothing cashes out, nothing is tradeable.
- "AI voice acting" — **wrong.** Cut for v1; residents are read, not
  heard.
- Real SF business names — everything in-world is a parody name;
  a piece naming a real Mission business as "in the game" is wrong.
