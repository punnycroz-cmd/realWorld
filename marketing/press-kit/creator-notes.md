# Creator & streamer notes — covering Real World

> For streamers, video essayists, and community creators. Different rules
> than press: you don't need permission to cover the game, but the accuracy
> rules below keep you (and us) honest. Nothing here is published yet —
> this file ships in the kit so it's ready the moment launch is approved.

## Why this game is unusually streamable

- **Watching is free, always.** Your entire audience can open the same
  block you're on and follow along live — no paywall between your stream
  and the thing you're streaming.
- **The public request feed is a built-in second screen.** Every paid
  intervention posts to a feed anyone can read, with attribution. Chat can
  audit exactly what you (or anyone) asked the world to do.
- **The mains can't be scripted.** The eight main characters are
  unpossessable — by players and by the developer. Whatever drama unfolds
  on your stream is genuinely unwritten; you can't be accused of staging it.
- **Quiet stretches are part of it.** The feed reports quiet honestly.
  Slow TV rules apply — put it on a second monitor and let the block breathe.

## Good formats

- **Watch party / co-stream** — ambient block + commentary; the feed as chat's ticker.
- **Request stream** — viewers suggest requests; you file them live and the
  feed shows the queue, the caps, the refunds in real time.
- **Recap / "week on the block"** — the feed is public history; recap
  episodes cut themselves.
- **Move-in arc** — create a character, rent, work, climb toward landlord
  on camera. Rent is real in-world stakes; eviction is possible.

## Rules of the block (for coverage)

1. **Label it "in development"** while that label is on the screenshots and
   the site. The build you see is the build you're showing.
2. **Attribution:** "Real World (in development) — [STUDIO NAME]" on
   screenshots and clips pulled from the kit.
3. **Don't imply endorsement or partnership** unless we've actually agreed
   to one. "Checking out" ≠ "sponsored by."
4. **Respect the fiction.** Residents are fictional; addresses are
   generated above real block ranges and can never be a real door. Don't
   dox the geography — enjoy it.
5. **Pricing talk:** cite figures as provisional until launch (see
   `copy-deck.md` § pricing phrasing).
6. **The possession ban is the story.** If you explain one mechanic, make
   it that one — it's what makes the drama trustworthy.

## Assets you can use

- `screenshots/` — thumbnails and overlays, with attribution + "in
  development" label.
- `keyart/` — hero images for videos and posts.
- `banners/` — sized for X/YouTube/Discord/LinkedIn headers; built for
  our own channels but fine for fan/community spaces with attribution.
- `logos/` — the lit-window mark; don't recolor or redraw it.
- `copy-deck.md` — approved wording if you want to describe the mechanics
  without accidentally promising a feature that doesn't exist.
