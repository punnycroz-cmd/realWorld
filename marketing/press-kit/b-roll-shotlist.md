# B-roll shotlist — Real World ("The Mission")

**Who this is for:** video press, streamers, and creators who want moving
footage of the game for a segment or video — and for our own capture
sessions. Pair with `creator-notes.md` (coverage rules) and
`guided-tour.md` (the talking points behind each stop).

**Status:** all shots below are capturable from the current development
build's spectator/director camera. Every clip must carry the
"development build" label — the harness HUD (top bar, character panel,
camera key row) is dev chrome, not the shipped spectator view. If you
capture with HUD visible, say so on screen.

**Camera keys visible in the dev harness** (for our own capture rigs):
`V` top/street · drag look · `Q/E` rotate · `C` director mode · `O` orbit ·
`Z/X` lens · scroll dolly · `WASD` fly · `R/F` up/down · `G` cutaway ·
`T` track resident · `1–9` camera marks · speed `1×–16×`, pause `II`.

## Optional color shots

- **Interior vignette** — café or flat behind the storefront glass
  (`G` cutaway). Pairs with `screenshots/v16-int-*.png`.
- **Dusk lamps** — sodium pools coming on at civil dusk; shoot the same
  corner at 16:30 and after dark for a time-cut.
- **Before/after** — intercut `screenshots/v1-A/B.png` stills with the
  current build for a "how far it's come" beat.
- **Night watch** — the feed at 3am: the block is quiet but not stopped.
  This is the persistence claim, proved.

## Capture rules

1. **Nothing staged beyond requests.** Every shot above is emergent —
   residents, weather, and feed events are the sim, not a scripted scene.
   Requests you place to enable a shot (weather, an event) are real
   requests; say so if asked.
2. **Label it.** "Development build" on screen or in the description.
   Harness UI visible = mention it's dev chrome.
3. **No fake outcomes.** Don't cut footage to imply a request succeeded
   when it was declined or queued — the feed is public and checkable.
4. **Names are fine.** Residents' first names and the parody venues
   (El Farolote, Mudhaus, etc.) are canon. Real SF business names do not
   exist in this world — don't add them in VO.
5. **Ambience sells it.** Room tone, fog quiet, café murmur — the
   animatic scratch beds in `trailer/out/*-scratch.mp4` show the intended
   mix level. Ship score is a licensing task (see TRAILER-PLAN §5).
