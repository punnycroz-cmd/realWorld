REAL WORLD
The Mission — one-sheet

Persistent life sim · browser · pre-launch

A neighborhood that's alive whether you're watching or not.

Real World is a browser-based "Truman Show" on real streets around Dolores Park in San Francisco's Mission District. Twenty-eight fictional residents live around the clock. Watching is free, always — players pay only for agency.

How it works

  • Watch free. The residents, the drama, the public request feed — the spectator product is the core, not a demo.
  • Request. Paid agency is time-boxed: declare an action and duration, pay credits upfront, hit a hard cap, watch it land on a public feed everyone can read.
  • Create a character. Player characters rent, work, and climb the ladder — tenant, owner, landlord of a second unit.

The hook

  • The eight mains can never be possessed — by players or by the game's owner. Storylines stay authorial; secrets stay sealed.
  • Deliberate cuts: no voice/TTS, no loot boxes, no cash-out/RMT, no crypto.
