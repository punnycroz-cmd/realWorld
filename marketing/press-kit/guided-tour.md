# Guided tour — the 10-minute press preview

> Run sheet for live walkthroughs (call or screen-share) once a preview
> build exists. Until then this doubles as the "what we'd show you" list —
> every stop maps to a screenshot or page in this kit. Owner approval is
> required before any preview happens; nothing here is public.

## Before the call

- [ ] Owner has approved this specific outlet/date (embargo terms per
  `PRESS-OUTREACH.md` §3 — one stated datetime, agreed *before* sharing).
- [ ] Preview build is labeled "development build" on screen; dev HUD
  either hidden or explained ("that header is our debug bar, not UI").
- [ ] Demo/seeded feed is clearly badged — never present a scripted demo
  feed as live world output. Say "this is seeded sample data" out loud.
- [ ] Have `fact-sheet.html`, `captions.txt`, and `copy-deck.md` open in
  tabs. Have this file open in another.
- [ ] Fallback: if the build won't run, tour the site instead — the demo
  page, the wire strip on the homepage, and this kit's screenshots cover
  every stop below.

## The route (10 minutes)

| # | Stop | Show | Say (approximate) |
|---|------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | The block, top-down (0:00–1:30) | The neighborhood overhead — real Mission street grid, residents mid-routine | "This is a real block of San Francisco — real streets, real Dolores Park. Everyone you see is fictional, and so is every house number." |
| 2 | Follow one resident (1:30–3:00) | Street-level follow-cam behind a main character | "The eight mains have full AI minds — jobs, rent, routines, secrets. Nobody controls them. Not players. Not us." |
| 3 | The request feed (3:00–4:30) | Public feed of filed requests, statuses visible | "Every paid intervention in this world posts here with the requester's name attached. The transparency is the anti-grief mechanic." |
| 4 | File a request, live (4:30–6:30) | The request form: action + duration + price, then watch it screen | "Agency is sold by the minute. You declare what you want and for how long, it prices it, caps it, and the world screens it — you get a screened opportunity, never mind control." |
| 5 | Money, honestly (6:30–7:30) | Pricing page / credits explainer | "Watching is free forever. Credits are roughly a cent each, non-transferable, no cash-out, no loot boxes. All figures provisional until launch." |
| 6 | Tenant → landlord (7:30–8:30) | A character's housing/lease view if available, else the cast page | "Players who move in start as tenants on in-world wages. Miss rent and you can be evicted. Climb far enough and you're the landlord." |
| 7 | The Archive (8:30–9:30) | History browser — pick a past day, replay the feed | "Everything is logged. The archive is the catch-up mechanism — 'what happened on your street while you slept' is a first-class feature." |
| 8 | Q&A buffer (9:30–10:00) | — | Point every mechanics question at `press-qa.md`; if it's not in there, the honest answer is "not decided yet." |

## Hard rules for the presenter

1. **Never promise possession of the mains.** If asked "can I play as
   them?" the answer is "no — that's the whole point."
2. **Never call the feed "live" on a demo build.** "Seeded sample data"
   or "recorded session" only.
3. **Never quote a final price.** "Provisional" is mandatory vocabulary.
4. **Don't oversell the AI.** The cast is authored; the AI performs it.
   "The AI decides what the character does next, inside rules and a
   character sheet we wrote."
5. **No roadmaps by implication.** Voice, mobile app, more neighborhoods —
   all undecided or cut. If it isn't in the fact sheet, it isn't promised.
6. **Names are parody.** If an outlet asks whether a real Mission business
   appears: streets and the park are real; every business is a parody name;
   every address is generated above the real block range.
