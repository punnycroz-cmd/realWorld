# What's New — build highlights for coverage

For "what changed" pieces, update posts, and second-look coverage. Each
entry names the renderer build the screenshots encode — the filename is
the build (`screenshots/v55-*.png` = build v55). Everything below is
verifiable in the current captures; nothing here is a promise about
future work.

Current development build: **v55** (September 2026).

---

## The short version

Three passes since the last kit refresh:

- **v53 — street furniture & lawn life.** The ground level got lived-in:
  hydrants, trash cans, news boxes, bike racks at the curb; picnic
  blankets on the Dolores lawns; the palm allée and worn desire-lines
  in the park.
- **v54 — the facade pass.** Every frontage got dressed: window boxes
  with geraniums, sleeve AC units with rust weeps, Juliet rails,
  house-number plates, mailbox rows, stoop pots, sidewalk A-boards.
- **v55 — the September turn.** The neighborhood knows what month it
  is: ginkgo crowns go gold while others rust and wine, mow stripes
  follow the lawn contours, gusts shake loose a drizzle of leaves,
  litter speckles the sidewalks, and the palms drop a frond or two.

## Why the season pass matters (the quotable bit)

> The neighborhood has a calendar, not just a clock. September in the
> Mission means the ginkgos turn first, the park lawns show their mow
> lines, and the wind has something to move. The world changes under
> the residents whether anyone watches or not — that's the whole
> premise, rendered.

The leaf-turn is deterministic per tree and driven by the in-world
month — it's a simulated season cue, not a one-off art swap. The same
mechanism will carry the neighborhood through the rest of the year.
