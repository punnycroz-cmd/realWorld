# Pitch Emails — tailored outreach drafts by desk

> **STATUS: TEMPLATES ONLY. Nothing here has been sent.**
> Sending requires the owner's explicit go (see
> `../PRESS-OUTREACH.md` for the outlet-list template and send rules).
> Brackets `[like this]` are fill-in slots — a pitch with an unfilled
> slot must never leave the building. One pitch per outlet; one polite
> follow-up maximum, after 7 days; then stop.

## Universal rules (apply to every draft)

- Personalize the first line — reference a specific piece the writer
  published. If you can't name one, this outlet isn't ready.
- Attach nothing. Link the press-kit zip path and the two strongest
  screenshots (`screenshots/v81-D.png`, `screenshots/v81-A.png`).
- Always include the credit line: "Real World (in development)".
- Offer the embargo pre-brief (`embargo-briefing.md`) to long-lead
  outlets only; same-day coverage gets the deadline desk
  (`deadline-desk.md`).
- Never promise a review build, keys, or dates that aren't in
  `../LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`. "In development" is the whole answer to
  "when."
