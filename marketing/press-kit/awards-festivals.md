## How to read fit

Real World's natural categories: innovation/technical-excellence juries
(social substrate, possession-ban design), sim/strategy showcases, and
"Most Anticipated" / upcoming-games slots. It's a hard sell for
narrative-award juries (no scripted story) and a poor fit anywhere that
requires a shipped game before launch.
