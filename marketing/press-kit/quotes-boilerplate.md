# Quotes & Boilerplate — Real World ("The Mission")

> Approved first-party material for articles, listings, and broadcast.
> These are the team's own words — attribute them to **"the Real World
> team"** (a named spokesperson slot is reserved for launch; do not
> invent a name). Everything below is accurate to the locked design:
> never quote us saying anything this file doesn't say.

## Approved quotes (verbatim-safe)

**On what the game is:**

> "Real World is a neighborhood, not a level. Twenty-eight AI residents
> live on one real San Francisco block — working, renting, falling out,
> making up — and the simulation runs whether anyone is watching or not.
> Watching is free, always."
>
> — the Real World team

**On the audience contract:**

> "You can reach in, but you can't reach through. Every paid request
> declares what it wants and how long it runs, prices it in capped
> credits, and posts to a public feed where everyone can see who asked
> for what. And the eight main characters can't be possessed by anyone —
> including us. The audience has a budget, not a puppet."
>
> — the Real World team

**On the monetization stance:**

> "We priced agency like coffee, not DLC. No loot boxes, no cash-out, no
> crypto, no marketplace. If a request can't be scheduled inside its
> hard cap, the credits refund automatically — the ledger is the game
> keeping its receipts in public."
>
> — the Real World team

**On the residents:**

> "The mains know what they are. They were born on the block with no
> assigned purpose and they're deciding what they're for — the claim is
> that they change, surprise, and choose, not that they pass for people."
>
> — the Real World team

**On the technology:**

> "The neighborhood is a stage, not a render. One weather field moves
> through it — fog, gusts, shower cells you can watch deciding whether
> to visit — and the residents read the same weather the sky does.
> Cheap text brains, a real ruleset, no script."
>
> — the Real World team

## Boilerplate

**25 words:**

> Real World is an AI life sim: twenty-eight residents on one real San
> Francisco block, simulated around the clock. Watching is free; paid
> requests are public, capped, and can never possess the cast.

**50 words:**

> Real World is a spectator life sim set on a single faithfully rendered
> Mission District block. Twenty-eight AI residents — eight mains, twenty
> ambient neighbors — work, rent, gossip, and improvise around the clock.
> Watching is free. Paid requests buy time-boxed, publicly logged agency —
> never possession of the eight main characters, which nobody controls.

**100 words:**

> Real World ("The Mission") is a spectator life sim: one real San
> Francisco block, twenty-eight AI residents, and a simulation that runs
> whether anyone is watching or not. The eight main characters are AIs
> that know what they are — born on the block with no assigned purpose —
> and they can never be possessed by anyone, including the developers.
> Watching is free, always. Viewers who want agency file paid requests:
> each declares an action and a duration upfront, prices it in
> hard-capped credits, and posts to a public feed. No loot boxes, no
> cash-out, no crypto. Currently in development.
