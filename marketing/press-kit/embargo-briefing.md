# Embargo Briefing — long-lead pre-brief book

For journalists and creators covering *Real World* under embargo ahead of
launch. This is the document we send **before** an embargoed preview
session: what you'll see, what's on the record, and the terms. On a
deadline instead? Use `deadline-desk.md`. Being shown the build live?
The presenter runs `guided-tour.md`; this book is what you read the
night before.

Everything here is accurate to the locked design and the current
development build (renderer v67). Brackets mark placeholders.

---

  for everyone (it usually doesn't; we won't punish the room for one
  break).

## The two-hour embargo session

We run the preview in four acts. You can follow along in the build or
just watch the shared screen — both are fine.

| Act | Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — The watch | 25 | We open the neighborhood cold. Top-down over the block, a resident inspector opened on a main character mid-routine. The point lands fast: this runs whether you're here or not. |
| 2 — The reach-in | 30 | A request, end to end: declared action + duration, priced in credits upfront, hard cap, posted to the public feed. Then a second request that queues — you'll see the auto-classification (exclusive/compatible/queued) work live. |
| 3 — The ladder | 30 | A player character's life: renting, working, the tenant→owner→landlord climb on a second unit. What the admin "landlord" is (a system role, not playable) and why. |
| 4 — The ban | 35 | The possession ban, demonstrated: we show that there is no control surface for the eight mains — not for players, not for us. Then open Q&A and free exploration. |

Ask for anything twice. The sim is persistent; if we can't produce a
moment on demand, that's a fact worth printing too.

## On the record

- Everything in `fact-sheet.html`, `copy-deck.md`, this book, and the
  kit documents — verbatim-safe.
- Design commitments: free watching; time-boxed paid requests with hard
  caps and a public feed; two currencies that never convert (credits are
  meta, game dollars are earned in-world); the possession ban on all
  eight mains; the tenant→owner→landlord ladder.
- Deliberate cuts, stated as cuts: no voice acting/TTS, no loot boxes,
  no cash-out or real-money trading, no crypto.

## Off the record / not for publication

- **Pricing numbers beyond "~1¢ per credit, provisional."** Credit
  pricing is unsettled; print it as planned/provisional or not at all.
- **Unannounced neighborhoods.** "The Mission" is the first
  neighborhood; the design supports more. Anything else is speculation —
  ask on the record and we'll tell you what's decided.
- **Internal cost figures.** We'll talk about the *shape* of the
  economics (inference is cheap; moderation and writing are the real
  cost). Internal numbers stay internal.
- **The moderation playbook detail.** We'll describe the anti-grief
  model (hard caps, public feed, classification, hiring rules for
  ambients). Operational thresholds stay undisclosed so they can't be
  gamed.

## The questions you'll want to ask (and can)

Pre-answered in `press-qa.md` — including the hard ones: "Is this an AI
slop machine?", "What stops a griefer with a wallet?", "Why would anyone
watch?", "Isn't this just Twitch Plays with nicer graphics?" Bring the
hostile versions; the answers are the story.
