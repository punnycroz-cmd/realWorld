The Truman Show you can visit.

Eight main characters — baristas, a nurse, a courier, a cook, a seamstress, a hardware-store landlord — run on full AI minds with memory, jobs, relationships, and secrets of their own. Watch

Per-viewer cameras: follow any resident, hold the Dolores Park overlook, the street lens on 18th & Guerrero, or the rooftop over the park. The spectator feed narrates the block in real time.

Reach in

File requests into the real pipeline — weather, street events, a hire of your own. Everything is screened, billed in credits, reviewed by the desk, and resolved in public on the Wire.

Move in

Create a character, sign a lease on a real Mission address, pay rent in game dollars that never mix with credits. The ledger keeps the books; the Wire keeps the story.
