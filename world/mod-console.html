    Shift report — aggregate only

    Counts and rates only. Individual appeals, request texts, and player handles never appear here — the public feed sees aggregates or nothing (appeal_flow.feed_visibility). Merge target: the monthly transparency recap marketing publishes.

    Flag roster — account standing

    Every account carrying flag weight, sorted by rolling score. Tiers per moderation.json account_flags: 3 → all requests human-reviewed 7 d · 6 → request privileges suspended 72 h · 9 → owner docket. Decay is −1 per clean 30 days. Flags are internal only — never shown publicly, never monetized around. A score of 9+ lands on the owner docket below; the console recommends, it never executes an account action.

    Writers’ docket — observed AI behavior, filed for the writers’ room

    The one constructive outlet for the locked boundary: there is no “report the AI” surface and no retcon tool. An incident filed here touches nothing — not the world, not the feed, not the player, not the character. It lands in the writers’ room queue; the writers decide what, if anything, changes on the page — never the console. File the AI’s own behavior only: if what you saw came from a player’s request, decide the request — that’s the queue’s job. Character names come from the reviewer whitelist; you cannot cite a secret you cannot see.
