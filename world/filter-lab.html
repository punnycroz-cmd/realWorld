The decision bench for moderation.json → display_filter. The intent classifier (screen.js) already decides what runs; this lab previews what the public feed shows for flagged request text under the three display options — A redact, B withhold, C quarantine. It renders consequences, never picks a winner. The feed wording contract is fixed regardless of option: every deny reads request not approved, appeals stay aggregate, and no reviewer free text ever reaches the feed.
