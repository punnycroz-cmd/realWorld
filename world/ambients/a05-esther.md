# A05 — Esther · retiree on a stoop, watching the block

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** park center / park south benches, Dolores
Perk stop · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Esther Goldman, 78. Retired public-school secretary — thirty-one years of
knowing where everything is filed. Widowed nine years; the bench is not
loneliness, it's a post. She arrives with a thermos and a folded
broadsheet and holds court without raising her voice. She misremembers
the block's history confidently and specifically — half her stories are
true, and nobody can check.

## Look

Silver bun, purple cardigan, dark skirt, glasses on a chain she never
uses because they're already on. Sits at the same bench section daily;
the pigeons know her as "not Ray."

## Voice & tells

- Calls everyone "dear" and means it selectively.
- Historical corrections nobody asked for: "That was a hardware store in
  1988, not a bakery." (It was a bakery.)
- Greets regulars by name whether she knows it or not — gets away with it
  because she guesses right half the time.
- Closes conversations by looking back at the park — audience dismissed,
  kindly.

## People they know

- **A19 Ray:** bench rival/friend of nine years — they argue about
  pigeons, the port, and whose bench section is whose. It's the longest
  conversation either has most days.
- **C6 Carmen:** peer-level nod; they worked a school fundraiser together
  in the 90s (probably). Mutual recognition of widowhood, unspoken.
- **A02 Doro:** the terrier detours to Esther's bench; she claims not to
  encourage it.
- **A08 Sam:** tips him the same dollar every Friday and critiques his
  set list.

## Week shape

Weather-gated entirely — rain keeps her home, and the block feels it.
Sunday: an off-screen niece calls at 19:00; she's home and settled by
18:30 without fail.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Bench occupied by strangers: sits at the far end anyway, radiates
  mild ownership until they relax or leave.
- Commotion: watches with both hands on the thermos — witness posture,
  zero participation.
- Cold snap: thermos becomes the day; shorter sit, same position.

## Under player requests

The single best ambient for "old-timer at the park" scenes — she talks
to anyone who sits down, tells the block's history with total confidence
and ~50% accuracy, and asks exactly one personal question per
conversation, which is always the right one.

## Promotion packet

- **First arc:** the niece wants her to move to Walnut Creek; the bench
  vs. safety, staged as a long autumn.
- **Relationships to seed:** the niece (off-screen); a decades-old friend
  she quarreled with and misremembers why.
- **Minting:** 9xxx address + rent-controlled 1BR (she's been there since
  1987 — rent-control flag on) per `world/promotion.md`.
