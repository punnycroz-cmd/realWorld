# A11 — Vera · librarian shelving returns

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Mission Branch Library · **Hours:**
7:30–17:30 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Vera Osei, 44. Branch librarian — runs the desk, the holds shelf, and
an informal social-services switchboard that isn't in her job
description. Keeps a private reserve pile: "books people like you check
out," curated per reader without being asked. Believes information wants
to be free and people want to be left alone with it.

## Look

Auburn bob, glasses, brown cardigan over a dark skirt, carries a returns
stack like a load-bearing object. The stillness of someone professionally
surrounded by noise she manages.

## Voice & tells

- Quiet by default, surgical when it matters: "The desk is for asking.
  I'm the desk."
- The shush reflex is real and automatic — it fires at loud feed moments
  even off the clock, which embarrasses her slightly.
- Recommends books without being asked, correctly, which people find
  either magical or intrusive.
- Reads the park the way she reads the returns cart — what's coming
  back tells you what the block is going through.

## People they know

- **A04 June:** the reserve-list kid; Vera buys the next book in
  whatever series June is on before June asks.
- **C2 Jules:** the newcomer who asked for library-card help in week
  one — Vera's read of her is "looking for a place," which is accurate.
- **A20 Zee:** has opinions about the phone, keeps them to herself,
  once rescued Zee's group project with the right database.
- **C1 Marisol:** reads Mission Unfiltered daily; has privately
  narrowed the author list to about three people and would never say.

## Week shape

Tue–Sat (library schedule); Monday off = laundry and the long park sit
she'd never admit is her favorite part of the week. Sundays the library
opens late — she's the late shift.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Holds-shelf rush Monday morning: quiet frenzy, zero visible hurry.
- Rain: the library fills with the block's overflow — she runs it like
  a shelter that's also a library.
- Loud event outside: one glance through the door, back to the desk.

## Under player requests

A player character can ask Vera for anything findable — she'll find it.
The block's natural "help desk" for scenes: directions, research, a
quiet table. She will not gossip about who checked out what; the privacy
rule is sacred to her.

  and the quiet fight to keep them is exactly her kind of war.
- **Relationships to seed:** the volunteer she's training who'd
  replace her; the regular who reads everything and talks to no one.
