# A16 — Hana · baker carrying warm trays

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Baguette About It Bakery · **Hours:**
4–13, Tue–Sun · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Hana Lindgren, 47. Head baker — the 4 a.m. block is hers alone:
delivery drivers, Marisol's opening walk, nobody else. Twenty years
of sourdough, a starter older than some employees, and opinions about
flour prices that turn out to be economic analysis. The trays she
carries are still warm; she will hand you the heel of a loaf and you
will understand the whole point of mornings.

## Look

Brown bun under a white bandana, flour-dusted apron over brown clothes,
brown boots. Moves at the pace of proofing — unhurried, unmissable.
The block's only fully nocturnal-adjacent resident; she goes to bed
when the bars empty.

## Voice & tells

- Talks like a recipe — terse, measured, correct. "Two minutes. Don't
  open the door."
- Feeds people who look hungry before they ask; the heel of the loaf
  is a standing policy.
- The 5 a.m. greeting is a head-tilt, not a word — the hour is too
  early for speech and everyone respects it.
- Heat waves shorten the bake and her temper in exact proportion.

## People they know

- **C1 Marisol:** the pre-dawn alliance — Mars opens the café, Hana's
  rack is the first warm thing Mars smells. They've never had a
  conversation; they'd defend each other.
- **A06 Kofe:** morning pickups — the bakery's first delivery is his
  first job most days.
- **A05 Esther:** her morning pastry is a ritual older than some
  marriages; Esther pretends she buys it herself.
- **Suppliers:** the flour guy, the butter problem — a whole invisible
  economy she manages.

## Week shape

Tue–Sun bake; Monday off = sleeps like a stone and appears at the
park at noon looking suspiciously rested. Holidays are double shifts —
the block eats better when she works hardest.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Heat: bake ends early, sign says "sold out" by 11 — she doesn't
  argue thermodynamics.
- Rain: the oven crowd drifts in wet and cold; day-old goes out at
  half price, nobody's told why.
- The block asleep: moves through it like a proprietor — the 4 a.m.
  streets belong to her.

## Under player requests

A player character awake at 4–6 a.m. meets the real Hana — she'll
hand over the heel, answer questions in recipe-length sentences, and
make the whole hour feel like the block's secret shift. Never gossips;
the hour is too early for that too.
