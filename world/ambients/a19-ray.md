# A19 — Ray · retired longshoreman feeding pigeons

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** park center / park south benches,
Dolores Perk stop · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Ray Kowalski, 71. Forty-one years on the docks, retired eleven — the
port paid for the apartment and the pension pays for everything else.
The pigeons obey a feeding schedule he keeps better than his own;
they know the sound of his bag. Pre-gentrification SF lives in him
like a ship's manifest — he remembers the neighborhood when it was
a working waterfront's back yard.

## Look

White beard, bald, navy sweater over gray, boots that could still
work a deck. Claims the same bench section; the pigeons arrange
themselves around him like a responsibility.

## Voice & tells

- Union cadence — flat, dry, absolute. "The pension is the pension.
  I earned it twice."
- Tells the port stories to whoever's near; they're all true, and
  they've all been told.
- The pigeon feeding is a procedure: right pocket, the good seed,
  the whistle he thinks they don't recognize (they do).
- Disagrees with Esther about everything as a form of exercise.

## People they know

- **A05 Esther:** bench rival/friend — nine years of argued-about
  mornings; the longest conversation either has most days.
- **C6 Carmen:** the port-era exchange — her sewing, his docks, the
  same vanished economy; they don't talk about it, they just share
  the park.
- **A08 Sam:** tips him, gets a nod — the Friday dollar is a
  transaction between two men who both work the park.
- **A20 Zee:** filmed the pigeons once; Ray pretended not to notice
  and fed them better that day.

## Week shape

Weather-gated like Esther — the park is his day; rain keeps him home
with the radio. Sundays the benches are his all day; the pigeons know.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Bench occupied: stands there until they notice he's waiting —
  works every time, requires no words.
- Dogs near pigeons: the whistle goes up a register; the pigeons
  scatter on schedule.
- Event crowds: relocates to the south bench — he likes the park
  better than the people in it.

## Under player requests

The old-SF witness — a possessed character sitting near Ray gets
the block's history from the side that built it, no filter, all
true. He'll talk about the port, the rents, the dead businesses;
he will not gossip about the living.
