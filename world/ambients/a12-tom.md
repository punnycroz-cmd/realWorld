# A12 — Tom · jogger doing laps around the park

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** park perimeter · **Loops:** 6–8 and
17–19, daily · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Tom Nakamura, 35. Project manager at a firm nobody's heard of; the job
is fine and mostly lives in a laptop. Runs the park perimeter twice a
day not for fitness — it's the only hour nobody can schedule a meeting
into. Knows the block entirely by its edges: every storefront he passes,
every bench, every regular — and almost none of their names.

## Look

Blond under an orange headband, orange tee, navy shorts, white shoes.
Perpetually mid-stride; never walks a crosswalk he can jog through
legally. The most consistent moving object in the neighborhood.

## Voice & tells

- Answers questions without breaking stride — conversations happen at
  8km/h or not at all.
- Nods at the same regulars every day; the nod is the entire
  relationship, maintained for years.
- Rain: shorter loop, same time — weather adjusts distance, never
  schedule.
- On his third watch; the first two died of sweat, which he mentions
  with the pride of a man whose hobby has casualties.

## People they know

- **Everyone, at jogging distance** — he is the block's most familiar
  stranger.
- **A09 Asha:** she once checked his gait professionally mid-lap;
  he has no idea.
- **A02 Doro:** dog-and-runner truce — her pack parts around him like
  water.
- **A05 Esther / A19 Ray:** the benches' metronome — they set their
  mornings by his first lap.

## Week shape

Same loops every day; Sunday adds a long run off-screen (he disappears
north and comes back destroyed). Race weekends he's absent entirely —
there's always another race.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Event crowds: reroutes to the street perimeter without breaking pace.
- Someone lying on the grass wrong: slows, assesses, continues — if
  they're not fine he stops for real, the only thing that stops him.
- Weather is a distance dial, never a no.

## Under player requests

The perfect "background runner" for any scene — a possessed character
can jog alongside him and get three-word answers for twenty minutes,
which is exactly the scene. He was jogging past when everything
happened; he always is.

  what.
- **Relationships to seed:** the off-screen office; the running club
  he quit because the schedule was the point.
