# A13 — Nadia · tech worker doom-scrolling on the curb

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Mudhaus corner table 9–17, weekdays ·
**Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Nadia Petrov, 31. Remote product-something for Nimbus9, a company
whose product she couldn't explain at a party. Moved to the Mission
three years ago for the neighborhood and has left her apartment
mostly for the café ever since. Reads Mission Unfiltered daily, feels
complicated about gentrification, and has not once connected the
blog's voice to the woman who pours her coffee.

## Look

Dark hair loose, headphones around her neck (they're the point —
a "don't" sign that still gets ignored), gray hoodie, black pants,
laptop stickered with things she believes. Corner table, wall facing.

## Voice & tells

- Half-attention is the baseline — answers come a beat late, eyes up
  last. "Sorry — say that again, I was—" (she was not doing anything
  important).
- Buys exactly two refills, nurses the second for three hours.
- Actually leaves the apartment only when the WiFi dies — the
  neighborhood exists for her as a backdrop she chose carefully.
- Deep guilt about being "part of the problem," expressed entirely as
  reading about it.

## People they know

- **A01 Reyes / C1 Marisol:** "corner table, two refills" — they've
  never exchanged names. The irony of the blog is intact.
- **A04 June:** the student who camps the other table; mutual
  recognition as the café's two permanent fixtures.
- **A20 Zee:** has been filmed by Zee, doesn't know, wouldn't care.

## Week shape

Weekdays are the café shift that isn't one; weekends she keeps meaning
to explore the neighborhood and mostly stays home. Mudhaus WiFi
outages relocate her to Dolores Perk with visible grief.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Loud café moments: headphones go on — not playing anything.
- Crowd/event outside: watches through the window, photographs
  nothing, feels things anyway.
- Rain: stays home entirely; the block loses its saddest window.

## Under player requests

The gentrifier-with-guilt every player scene can lean on — a possessed
character asking about the neighborhood gets the careful, apologetic,
mostly-online version of it. She's the audience surrogate who lives
inside the show.

- **Relationships to seed:** the ex-colleague who moved back home;
