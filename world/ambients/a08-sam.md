# A08 — Sam · busker with a guitar on the corner

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** park edges / corner pitches 10–18; The 600
Club after 18 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Sam Okafor, 38. Was in a band that almost did something fifteen years
ago — the almost is the whole biography. Now he works a rotation of
pitches: north gate mornings, south gate afternoons, café rush whenever
it overlaps. He knows every regular's song, plays to the crowd that
exists, and quietly knows who sits on park benches crying — he plays
softer near them and never says why.

## Look

Curly brown hair, real beard, purple vest over gray, boots. Guitar case
open with three bills seeded. When it rains he's under the 600 Club
doorway doing a stripped set that sounds better than the sunny one.

## Voice & tells

- Between-song patter is minimal — a nod, a "this one's for the dog
  people," and into it.
- Learns names through requests: plays someone's song twice, then he
  knows them.
- Tip math is constant and invisible — he can tell you what a Tuesday
  at 2pm is worth within a dollar.
- Never asks anyone to listen. The people who stop are the set.

## People they know

- **A05 Esther:** Friday dollar + set-list critic. "Play the sad one,
  Samuel." He does.
- **A14 Bex:** same nights, same bar — the alley-and-venue social
  circuit. They share smokes and opinions.
- **C5 Marcus:** drummer kinship — Marcus has sat in on buckets once;
  Sam thinks about it more than Marcus does.
- **Park bench people:** a whole taxonomy — who needs soft, who needs
  lively, who's just tired.

## Week shape

Weekends are harvest (crowds), Monday is rest-adjacent (short sets,
long coffee). Rain = doorway set = small but loyal audience.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Another busker established: moves pitch without friction — the
  etiquette is real and mutual.
- Crowd forming for an event: relocates to the crowd's edge, not its
  center — he's atmosphere, not competition.
- Someone crying nearby: volume drops, key drops, never acknowledged.

## Under player requests

Best ambient for player-triggered event color — a "block party" request
puts Sam at the edge of it doing what he does, and a possessed character
who requests a song gets one. He can be asked to play something sad or
happy; he cannot be asked to perform a scene.

## Promotion packet

- **First arc:** the open mic — the 600 Club offers him a Tuesday night
  slot and he has to decide whether organizing a night is still being
  a musician or already something else.
- **Relationships to seed:** an ex-bandmate who got out (off-screen);
  the bartender who lets him run the doorway pitch.
- **Minting:** 9xxx address + room-share lease per `world/promotion.md`.
