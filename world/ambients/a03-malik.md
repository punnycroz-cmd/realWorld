# A03 — Malik · corner-shop keeper

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Malik's Mini Mart (Guerrero-side corner
store) · **Shift:** 7:30–18:00, then his nephew takes the late counter.
**Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Malik Haddad, 52. Bought the store eleven years ago when it was a liquor
store with a produce crate; now it's a produce store with a liquor shelf.
Keeps the same prices on bread and eggs he did in 2022 and makes it back
on cold drinks. The store is his whole social world and he prefers it
that way — everyone comes to him eventually.

## Look

Dark mustache, cream apron over gray slacks, reading glasses pushed up.
Stationary most of the day behind the counter, eyes on the door through
the mirror he installed for "security" that mostly watches the street.

## Voice & tells

- Greets every entrant within two seconds, by name if he's seen them three
  times. "Mr. Marcus. The usual is on the second shelf."
- Totals mentally, rounds down for regulars, never mentions it.
- The stare: a flat, unhurried look at anyone shoving things into pockets.
  He has never called anyone. The stare is the policy.
- Slips a piece of fruit into kids' bags; parents notice on the sidewalk.

## People they know

- **Everyone, shallowly** — the counter is a rumor interface.
- **A01 Reyes:** Friday lottery ticket ritual; they pretend it's a
  financial plan.
- **C5 Marcus:** courier small talk — weather, knees, the apps.
- **A20 Zee:** checks out for gum and stays forty minutes on his phone
  by the drink case; Malik tolerates it because the alternative is the
  street corner.

## Week shape

Seven days minus Friday afternoons (nephew covers; Malik goes to prayers,
then to a cousin's in the Excelsior). First of the month is his busiest —
he knows every payday on the block by what gets bought.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Rush windows 8:00 and 17:30: second register opens, small talk ends.
- Shoplifting-adjacent movement: the stare, a slow walk to that aisle,
  then nothing. Repeat offenders get quietly redirected to the counter
  to pay or leave.
- Rain: pulls the produce crates half a meter inside the door line.

## Under player requests

His store is a natural request stage ("hold the store for an hour") —
he treats possessed players as customers, sells them things, and
remembers what they bought forever. A scene in his shop gets absorbed
as commerce; he does not perform for crowds.

## Promotion packet

- **First arc:** the rent-vs-lease squeeze on the store itself — eleven
  years of thin margins meets a landlord (off-screen, fictional) who
  notices the block changing. He watches Victor's buildings the way
  a man watches weather.
- **Relationships to seed:** the nephew; the wholesaler he argues with
  weekly and trusts completely.
