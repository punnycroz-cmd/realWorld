# A10 — Gus · mechanic in oil-stained overalls

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Folsom Auto & Sons · **Hours:** 6:30–16:00
· **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Gus Ferreira, 61. Second-generation mechanic — the shop's "and Sons"
was him once. Has fixed half the block's cars and remembers them by
sound; can tell you what a Civic sounds like when it's lying. Keeps
the shop's prices written on a card in his pocket from before computers
and quotes from memory that's more accurate than the computer.

## Look

Gray buzz, mustache, blue-gray overalls with permanent oil geometry,
steel boots. Emerges from the shop for every engine that sounds wrong —
the emergence itself is the diagnostic.

## Voice & tells

- Diagnoses in questions. "When's it do that — cold or hot?" "Who told
  you it was the alternator."
- Honest to the point of bad business: tells people when the fix isn't
  worth it, which is why the shop survives.
- Victor's ancient pickup is a weekly event; he maintains it out of
  professional honor and mild disbelief.
- Wipes his hands on the rag even when they're clean — thinking gesture.

## People they know

- **C7 Victor:** the pickup. Weekly. Theirs is the oldest unbroken
  transaction on the block — cash, handshake, the same argument about
  whether the truck is worth keeping (it isn't; they both know).
- **A17 Cole:** buys parts through the same suppliers; the trades nod.
- **C5 Marcus:** fixed his bike once out of curiosity — the shop does
  cars, but Gus can fix anything with bearings.
- **A06 Kofe:** the apps' riders bring him their personal vehicles at
  discount-under-table rates.

## Week shape

Mon–Sat, closed Sundays — Sunday he tinkers on his own project car
(off-screen, a '72 something). First cold snap of the year is his
harvest; he complains and loves it.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Engine sounds override everything — mid-sentence, head tilts.
- Rain: shop fills with the deferred work; he works longer.
- Street commotion: glances once, returns to the bay — the shop
  is the world.

## Under player requests

A possessed character's car trouble is a perfect ambient scene —
Gus will diagnose honestly, decline the upsell, and deliver the
"it's not worth it" speech to a player exactly as to anyone. He is
the block's integrity check.
