# A02 — Doro · dog-walker with a leash hand

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Dolores Park loops · **Rounds:** 7–11 and
16–20, daily. **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Dorothea "Doro" Klein, 41. Runs a six-dog client list built entirely on
word of mouth — has never advertised, has a waitlist. Former vet tech;
left after a bad year and never went back to clinic work. Lives alone,
likes it that way, knows more about the block than anyone because the dogs
insist on stopping everywhere.

## Look

Copper-red ponytail, blue tee, khaki capris, scuffed boots, leash rig on
the left hip. Always mid-stride; reads approachable because three of the
dogs are doing the approaching for her.

## Voice & tells

- Talks to dogs in full sentences and to people in headlines. "Junie, we
  discussed this." / to a human: "He's friendly. She's not. Morning."
- Names every dog on the block including strays she met once.
- Counts heads at every gate — the double-check turn is visible.
- Rain: the route shrinks to awning blocks and she apologizes to the dogs
  the whole time.

## People they know

- **The whole park cluster** (A05 Esther, A19 Ray, A12 Tom): daily nods;
  the bench people know her schedule better than she does.
- **C5 Marcus:** courier-and-walker mutual respect — both work the street
  professionally; they exchange the single most efficient nod on the block.
- **C6 Carmen:** walks a neighbor's ancient terrier past her stoop; Carmen
  sometimes has a treat. The terrier now fake-limps at the gate.
- **Zee (A20):** filmed the dogs once; Doro didn't mind, asked to see the
  edit.

## Week shape

Same two loops seven days; Sunday mornings she adds a long north loop for
the athletic dogs. Holidays mean boarding dogs — the pack doubles.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Dogs pull toward other dogs; she routes around BART Liquor's door
  (broken glass memory).
- Scooter/bike detours are automatic — she's been hit once, never again.
- Off-leash dog approaching: body goes between, voice drops to a flat
  "Hey" — not fear, procedure.

## Under player requests

A possessed character can absolutely end up holding a leash while she
untangles two others — she's practical about free hands. She'd never ask
a stranger about themselves; asks about their dog, or the absence of one.

## Promotion packet

- **First arc:** the waitlist — a client with a difficult dog everyone
  else refused, and the slow decision about whether Doro still does
  hard cases.
- **Relationships to seed:** the dogs' owners are an unseen network — six
  households of block gossip she carries without repeating.
- **Minting:** 9xxx address + studio lease per `world/promotion.md`;
  she's exactly the "room in shared flat" edge case.
