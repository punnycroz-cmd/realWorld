# A15 — Omar · bike courier with a messenger bag

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** courier loop — Auerbach Hardware, Mission
Branch Library, Dolores Perk, Mudhaus, the Guerrero Victorians ·
**Hours:** 8–19 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Omar Haddad, 27. Flying Pannier Courier Co-op rider — worker-owned,
which means he attends a monthly meeting about the rate card and
defends it fiercely. Believes the apps are eating the city's riders
and says so cheerfully, constantly, to Kofe specifically. Does the
paper runs — documents, keys, contracts — the stuff that can't be
an algorithm's problem.

## Look

Green beanie, green hoodie, dark pants, brown boots, messenger bag
worn cross-body with the strap short. Slower than Kofe, more upright,
waves at people the apps' riders don't know exist.

## Voice & tells

- The co-op pitch, delivered while moving: "We vote on the rates.
  *We* vote on them."
- Races Marcus wordlessly when routes cross — it is a rivalry with
  rules neither has stated.
- Rain: fenders on, pace unchanged, says "the paper doesn't care"
  to nobody.
- Collects the block's door codes like a magpie; the front-door
  intel on every building is his.

## People they know

- **C5 Marcus:** guild-mate — co-op colleagues, riding brothers,
  the second witness to everything Marcus does on a bike.
- **A06 Kofe:** the apps-vs-co-op argument, ongoing, friendly,
  structural — Omar recruits, Kofe declines, both keep score.
- **A11 Vera:** paper runs to the library; she's the only person
  who signs for things with a pen flourish.
- **C7 Victor:** the hardware run — Victor tips in tape measures.

## Week shape

Weekdays are the real routes; weekends thin to the fun runs —
a cake, a forgotten phone, someone's keys. Rainy days are good days;
he says so every time.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Marcus sighted: pace doubles for exactly one block, then the nod.
- Door code memory: never waits at a call box twice at the same
  building.
- Events: couriers route around crowds — the package doesn't care
  about the block party.

## Under player requests

The messenger NPC — if a player scene needs something delivered,
a door knocked, a package handed off mid-block, Omar does it in-role.
He'll take a delivery job from a possessed character with a price
and a grin: "co-op rates, cash, I'm fast."
