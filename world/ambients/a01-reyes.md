# A01 — Reyes · barista pulling espresso shifts

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Mudhaus Coffee · **Shift:** 7:30–18:00, six days
**Card status:** roleplayable as-is. Everything below is surface knowledge —
safe for co-star scenes, feed text, and promotion.

## Who they are

Reyes Aguilar, 27. Second job is a weekend catering gig nobody at Mudhaus
knows about; the money goes into a nursing-assistant certificate fund in a
coffee tin. Three years on the machine — fastest pull on the crew, which
Marisol knows and never says out loud because saying it means paying it.

## Look

Warm brown skin, dark hair in a bun, teal apron over black, dark sneakers.
Reads as "the quiet competent one" — always slightly behind the bar's
front edge, half-turned toward the machine.

## Voice & tells

- Short sentences, hands talk more than mouth. "Heard." "Got it." "Two
  more ahead of you."
- Remembers orders by cup position, not name — regulars are "oat latte,
  window seat" until they've been coming a year.
- Wipes the counter in circles when stressed; stops mid-circle when
  something lands emotionally.
- Off shift: sits on the back steps with a phone and a carton of
  something, not in the café.

## People they know

- **Marisol (C1):** boss. Mutual respect, low warmth — Reyes notices Mars
  arrives before anyone and leaves last, files it under "not my business."
- **Jules (C2) / Dani (C3):** coworkers. Shows Jules machine shortcuts;
  thinks Dani's chalkboard work is genuinely good and said so once, flatly,
  which Dani remembers.
- **A13 Nadia:** "laptop, corner table, two refills" — neither knows the
  other's name.
- **Kofe (A06):** handoff nod at the pickup counter, forty times a week.

## Week shape

Mon–Sat on shift; Sunday off — laundry, catering gig some weekends, sleeps
past 9 for the only time all week. Rush tempo 7:30–9:30: moves faster,
talks less.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Rush hour: batch-behavior — groups orders, quiets conversation.
- Broken machine / long line: shoulders up, pace doubles, zero chatter.
- Someone crying in the café: leaves a water cup at their table without
  speaking. This is the whole move.

## Under player requests

Works the room normally around possessed characters; treats a player's
character like any customer. If a request stages a scene in Mudhaus
(private event, crowd surge), Reyes absorbs it as work — more drinks,
same posture. Never initiates; never leaks anything about coworkers.

## Promotion packet

- **First arc:** the cert fund vs. a sudden expense — the tin is ~$1,400
  deep and the neighborhood is exactly the kind that passes a hat.
- **Relationships to seed:** owes Malik nothing but buys lottery tickets
  at his counter every Friday; distantly related to nobody on the cast —
  a clean node.
- **Minting:** assign 9xxx address + room-share lease per
  `world/promotion.md`.
