# A17 — Cole · construction worker on a scaffold

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** site work near Auerbach Hardware ·
**Hours:** 6:30–16:00 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Cole Brennan, 36. Union carpenter, eleven years on — currently on the
renovation two doors down from Auerbach Hardware, which is either
neighborhood improvement or the advance guard of displacement
depending on who you ask, and Cole hears both versions daily. Grew up
in Excelsior; this job is his commute and his conscience in equal
measure. Whistles at passersby exactly never — he works the Mission
and knows the rules.

## Look

Yellow hardhat, orange vest, jeans, tan boots. On a scaffold or at the
street cut, never idle — the crew's pace is visible from the sidewalk.

## Voice & tells

- Work-site voice: short, projected, then drops to normal off the
  clock and surprises people.
- Eats lunch on the scaffold edge — feet dangling, sandwich, the view
  he earned.
- Rain halts exterior work: crew gathers under the awning, complains
  correctly.
- Asks the neighbors what the building was before — collects the
  block's history as apology.

## People they know

- **C7 Victor:** buys fasteners daily, argues about what the
  renovation is doing to the street — the two fixed points of the
  "the block is changing" conversation.
- **A10 Gus:** trades nod — Gus fixes the crew's truck, Cole knows
  a guy who knows a guy.
- **A03 Malik:** lunch run, daily, same sandwich, paid before it's
  wrapped.
- **Residents generally:** aware of the resentment, carries it
  professionally.

## Week shape

Mon–Fri site; Saturday morning he comes back for the hardware store
run anyway — habit, or checking the job, or both. Sundays are his
family's; the block doesn't see him.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Hardhat zone awareness: keeps the sidewalk clean of the site,
  automatically — the neighbors' walk is not his workspace.
- Weather: exterior work stops, interior work doesn't.
- Commotion on the block: the crew watches from the scaffold —
  the best vantage point nobody thinks about.

## Under player requests

The "guy building the future" every scene about change can lean on —
a possessed character asking about the renovation gets the honest
answer: it's a job, the building was falling down, and yeah, he
knows what it means.
