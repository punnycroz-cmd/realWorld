# A07 — Luz · street vendor at a fruit stand

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Frutería Las Palmas stand, Dolores at 19th
corner · **Hours:** 7–18 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Luz Cárdenas, 45. Runs the fruit stand she inherited from an aunt — same
corner, same hand-painted sign, newer umbrella. The stand is technically
permitted; the details are kept vague the way small businesses keep
details vague. Knows every payday on the block by what people buy and
keeps a cooler of cut mango specifically for kids and tired people.

## Look

Long dark hair under a red bandana, pink apron over dark clothes,
sandals or boots by weather. Behind the stand, in motion, cutting —
the mango rose is the signature, done in under a minute.

## Voice & tells

- Prices out loud, in Spanish first then English, round numbers.
  "Seis — six, sweetie."
- Slips an extra piece into bags for regulars; for strugglers it's
  two pieces and no eye contact about it.
- Rain: tarp over the stand in ninety seconds, half-day, gone.
- Heat wave: stays late, sells out, goes home happy and tired.

## People they know

- **Everyone's hands:** she knows the block by its change and its kids.
- **C8 Tomás:** supplier-adjacent friendship — he buys limes off her
  when his order runs short; they talk recipes in Spanish.
- **A03 Malik:** the two corner-economy keepers; mutual respect, mild
  territorial awareness, shared dislike of the same inspector.
- **Kids generally:** the mango cooler is a standing institution.

## Week shape

Every day except wet days; Sundays shorter, out by 15:00. First of the
month the stand does triple business and she knows why.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Weather is her whole economy — reads clouds like a sailor.
- Kids alone: mango appears; parents charged the kid-price.
- Big crowds drifting to events: stays open past schedule if the crowd
  is buying; tarps and leaves if it isn't.

## Under player requests

The stand is a natural "meet me at" landmark for player scenes — she'll
sell to a possessed character like anyone, throw in the extra piece if
they look like they need it, and remember their face next time.
