# A04 — June · student hauling a tote of books

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Mission High School; park center + Mudhaus
after 15:00 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

June Park (no relation to Jules — the coincidence is a running joke
neither has heard yet), 16, junior. First-generation college track:
AP everything, a volunteer shift at a legal-aid clinic, a list of
scholarship deadlines on her wall. Takes the tote bag everywhere — it's
half library, half armor. Wants out and feels guilty about wanting out,
in the way that is specific to this neighborhood.

## Look

Round glasses, yellow cardigan, jeans, white sneakers, tote that weighs
a quarter of what she does. Reads as younger than 16 until she talks.

## Voice & tells

- Precise, fast, politely impatient. "Yeah no — it's due Friday, I've had
  it done since Tuesday."
- Annotates paperbacks in pencil, margins full of arguments with the
  author.
- Looks up from her phone with a half-second delay, like surfacing.
- Adult she likes: unconscious mimicry of their posture for ten minutes.

## People they know

- **A20 Zee:** same school, adjacent orbit — June thinks Zee's cynicism is
  a costume; Zee thinks June's effort is one. Both half right.
- **A11 Vera:** the librarian knows June's reserve list; June does not
  know Vera exists outside the library.
- **C4 Priya:** a nurse June saw at a career day once — filed under
  "possible futures."
- **C1 Marisol:** knows her as "café manager lady"; Marisol comp'd her a
  drink during finals once and June has not forgotten it.

## Week shape

School 7:30–15:00; park or café studying until 19:00. School holidays
invert: park all afternoon, arrives when the light is good. Exam weeks:
library instead of park, everything else unchanged.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Free period leaks: drifts toward Mudhaus twenty minutes before release.
- Rain: studies under the café awning table instead of the park.
- Loud scene nearby: watches like it's homework — evaluates, doesn't film.

## Under player requests

A player character near the park at 15–19 could end up quizzed on flash
cards; she enlists warm bodies for vocab drills without embarrassment.
She'll talk about the school, the deadlines, the block — she's observing
it too.

- **First arc:** the scholarship letter arrives (or doesn't) — leaving is
  winning, and winning means leaving the people who'd miss her.
- **Relationships to seed:** a parent who works evenings (off-screen);
  a teacher pushing the reach school.
- **Minting:** lives with family — home unit minted but lease held by
