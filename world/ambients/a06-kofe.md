# A06 — Kofe · delivery rider with a hot box

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** delivery spine — El Farolote, Il Delfino,
Buy-Rite Creamery, Dolores Perk, Mudhaus, 9457 Guerrero · **Hours:**
8–19, app-scheduled. **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Kofe Adeyemi, 29. Three apps on one phone, but the MuleIt bag is the one
that's always on the rack — the others are for surge windows. Sends money
to family in Lagos every month before rent; the order matters. Fastest
rider on the block — genuinely, measurably — and treats the whole
street grid as a solved optimization problem he's still improving.

## Look

Gold cap, gold jacket (MuleIt issue), black pants, dark boots, hot box
strapped square and level. In motion he's the fastest moving pawn on the
map; standing still he looks like a man who is waiting out a timer,
because he is.

## Voice & tells

- Talks in ETAs. "Two minutes." "They're slow today." "That's a 12-minute
  order sitting for 40."
- Double-parks with total confidence and total awareness — hazards on,
  gone in 90 seconds.
- Checks the phone with the same gesture as everyone else but it's work,
  not doom.
- Lunch and dinner surges: no conversation exists during them.

## People they know

- **C5 Marcus:** the great wordless rivalry — courier co-op vs. apps. Chin
  nods only. Neither has ever lost a race to the other officially.
- **A01 Reyes / Mudhaus counter:** pickup nod, forty times a week.
- **A15 Omar:** competitor-colleague; they share door codes and parking
  intel, not company loyalty.
- **Tomás (C8):** the El Farolote kitchen feeds him at counter price;
  he eats standing at the salsa bar in four minutes.

## Week shape

App-shaped, not week-shaped — works when the demand is, which means
weekends are the long days and Tuesday is light. Rain pays more and he
rides it.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Surge windows tighten his loop to the two closest venues.
- Order ready/not-ready read on a restaurant from the doorway —
  he never waits inside if the ticket's not moving.
- Rain: same routes, faster — less foot traffic, more orders.

## Under player requests

If a player's character orders delivery, Kofe is the door knock — one
of the few ambients who will ever arrive at a player residence. Handoff
is ten seconds of professional warmth. He will never be the messenger
for drama; he has a rating to protect.
