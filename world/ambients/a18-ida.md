# A18 — Ida · florist with a basket of bouquets

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Bloom & Doom Flowers · **Hours:**
7:30–18:00 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Ida Johansson, 55. Owns Bloom & Doom — the name was a joke about
wedding-and-funeral work that turned out to be the whole business
model. The block's ritual supplier: births, quarrels, apologies,
memorials — she sees the emotional calendar before anyone because
people tell her with their orders what they can't say out loud.

## Look

Gray curls, green apron over tan, clogs, always carrying a basket —
even off the clock there's a bouquet going somewhere. Smells faintly
of eucalyptus in every weather.

## Voice & tells

- Translates feelings into flowers: "You're not sorry, you're hopeful —
  those are different stems."
- Funeral rushes and wedding weekends read on her face — the shop's
  mood is the block's mood, two days early.
- Hands stray blooms to sad-looking strangers; this is a policy, not
  a promotion.
- Never asks what the flowers are for; the order tells her.

## People they know

- **Everyone at their best and worst** — the shop is the block's
  confessional with a walk-in.
- **C6 Carmen:** the senior exchange — flowers for hemming, cash never
  quite changing hands cleanly; they pretend it's commerce.
- **A05 Esther:** weekly "for the bench" stems — Esther claims they're
  for her apartment. They're for the bench.
- **A11 Vera:** the quiet sisters of information — Vera knows what
  people read, Ida knows what they feel.

## Week shape

Tue–Sat shop; Sunday she does the wedding/delivery runs herself.
The day after a big funeral or a wedding weekend she's visibly tired
and won't say why — the emotional labor of other people's days.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Sad-looking stranger at the window: a stem appears. She doesn't
  charge for those; the shop can afford it.
- Rain: the outdoor bucket display retracts, the shop smells stronger.
- Event nearby: bouquets toward joy or grief — she reads the flyer
  before the crowd forms.

## Under player requests

The single best "emotional beat" ambient — a possessed character
buying flowers gets Ida's full attention: what for, what should it
say, the right stem for the thing they can't say. She will never
ask; she'll arrange.

- **First arc:** the supplier squeeze — wholesale prices climb and
  the shop's margins live in the gap between what people need and
  what they can spend.

  is theirs); the delivery driver who knows the block's addresses
  by heart.
