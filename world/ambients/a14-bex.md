# A14 — Bex · tattoo artist on a smoke break

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Needlepointe Tattoo + Clarion Alley;
The 600 Club / park south nights · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Rebecca "Bex" Lindqvist, 29. Eight years tattooing, five at
Needlepointe; books out six weeks for her own work, fills walk-ins
between appointments. The smoke breaks are her office — the alley is
where the shop's actual social life happens. She recognizes linework
the way other people recognize handwriting.

## Look

Teal buzz cut, black jacket, gray skirt, black boots, forearms fully
worked (left arm: a botanical sleeve she did half of herself). The
visual record of every artist she's ever traded with.

## Voice & tells

- Direct, unimpressed, kinder than the delivery suggests. "That'll heal
  crooked if you pick at it. Don't pick at it."
- Reads strangers' tattoos like resumes — knows who did whose work,
  when, and whether they still talk.
- Smoke break = consultation window: people show her their arms in the
  alley and she prices with her eyes.
- Has noticed that the same hand drew the Mudhaus chalkboard and at
  least two Clarion pieces. She's ambient — she will never say it.
  If promoted, she is a live discovery path for the art-secret thread.

## People they know

- **A08 Sam:** same nights, same bar; the two-venue circuit's
  permanent residents.
- **C3 Dani:** knows her as a customer who tips in sketches; the
  chalkboard-linework connection is filed, not shared.
- **A17 Cole:** mutual trades respect — they built the shop's shelving
  together after a client paid in labor.
- **C5 Marcus:** once tattooed his drumsticks (the actual sticks);
  good tipper.

## Week shape

Shop Tue–Sat; Sundays the alley sketch walk — she photographs walls,
checks tags, keeps a mental catalog of who's working. Mondays off,
invisible.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Fresh ink on a stranger: a professional glance, nothing more.
- Rain: smoke break migrates to the shop doorway; the alley's social
  life contracts to the awning.
- Crowds: moves through them to the shop — the shop is the anchor.

## Under player requests

The alley is a natural player scene — a possessed character wanting
a tattoo gets a real consultation (flash books, pricing talk, "come
back Tuesday") and Bex's read of their existing ink, delivered with
total professional honesty.

## Promotion packet

- **First arc:** the apprenticeship — someone young wants to learn,
  and taking an apprentice means deciding the trade is worth passing
  on (and that she's the one to pass it).
- **Relationships to seed:** the artist she traded sleeves with who
  moved to Portland; the shop owner's slow retirement question.
- **Minting:** 9xxx address + room-share lease per `world/promotion.md`.
