# A09 — Asha · nurse in scrubs off a shift

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** SF General (east-edge commute) → Mudhaus
evenings · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.
**Name note:** code name was `Priya` in NV_CAST through v0 — collided
with C4 Priya Raman, also a nurse. Renamed to **Asha** in code at v1.

## Who they are

Asha Nair, 33. Night-rotating RN on a med-surg ward — four years in,
competent past the point where anyone checks on her, which is its own
problem. Lives alone in a studio she chose specifically because nobody
she knows lives on her floor. Comes to Mudhaus after shifts not for
coffee — for the ten minutes of being a person who is not a nurse.

## Look

Teal scrubs, white sneakers, dark ponytail, badge reel clipped low.
Post-shift she has the specific tiredness of someone whose job is being
needed; on off days she's in a hoodie and unrecognizable.

## Voice & tells

- Calm, low, asks questions that are technically not advice: "When did
  you last eat something that wasn't from the counter?"
- Instinctive first-responder drift toward anyone sitting badly on a
  bench — checks breathing with a glance, moves on if it's just tired.
- Off-shift she talks about anything except the hospital. If the hospital
  comes up, she gives the clean version.
- Laughs once a day, and whoever caused it doesn't know it counted.

## People they know

- **C4 Priya:** colleague-adjacent — different ward, overlapping shifts;
  they recognize each other on sight as the other nurse who lives nearby.
  Not friends; something more useful — they can read each other's bad
  weeks without talking.
- **Mudhaus crew:** "evening nurse" — Reyes sets her order going when
  she walks in.
- **A12 Tom:** the jogger she's professionally observed mid-lap — she
  knows his gait is fine. He doesn't know she checked.

## Week shape

Rotating — three or four long days then off days that are mostly sleep
and laundry. The block only sees the Mudhaus version of her: 19:30–21:00,
counter seat, phone face-down.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Post-shift: slowest walk home on the block — decompression pace.
- Bench distress: the drift-and-check, automatic.
- Crowd event noise: moves away — she has enough alarms at work.

## Under player requests

A possessed character who gets sick or hurt in a scene: Asha is the
ambient who notices first — she'll do a welfare check in character,
suggest water/sitting/the obvious, and decline to be dramatic about it.
She will never discuss patients.

  anything; the one friend from nursing school who left the field.
