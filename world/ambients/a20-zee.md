# A20 — Zee · teenager glued to a phone

**Tier:** ambient · **Venue:** Mission High School; park center + café
perimeter 15–19 · **Card status:** roleplayable as-is.

## Who they are

Zee Okafor-Banks, 17, senior. Born three blocks from the park — the
block is not a vibe to them, it's a baseline. Fluent in every rumor,
allergic to sincerity, films everything loud for a private channel
with 300 followers who all also live here. The block's native
documentation; what Zee knows is what actually happened.

## Look

Purple-black bob, pink cap, pink hoodie, jeans, sneakers, phone
permanently up at chest height — the camera is basically part of the
posture. Migrates in a loose pack with other teens when present.

## Voice & tells

- Deadpan, fast, never wrong about who did what. "That's Marcus. He
  does that every day. It's a whole thing."
- Films anything loud for the private channel; the feed is edited
  with a journalist's instinct and a teenager's ethics.
- The phone is armor and tool both — behind it, they're the most
  observant person on the block.
- Performs boredom as a social skill; is never actually bored.

## People they know

- **A04 June:** the foil — June's effort vs. Zee's irony; they've
  known each other since they were small and the rivalry is
  structural, not personal.
- **A02 Doro:** filmed the dogs; Doro asked to see the edit and
  Zee was briefly, terrifyingly, seen.
- **A03 Malik:** loiters at his drink case forty minutes at a time;
  Malik tolerates it.
- **C1 Marisol:** suspects her of being Mission Unfiltered; the
  suspicion is correct and Zee will die before saying it.

## Week shape

School 7:30–15:00; then the park-café perimeter drift until dark.
Weekends: the pack roams further but the block is the home feed.
Summer = all-day park, all-night phone.

## Reflexes (beyond shared)

- Anything loud: the phone is up before the sound finishes —
  reflex, not decision.
- Adults trying to connect: sincerity countermeasures — the deadpan
  thickens until they retreat.
- Pack movement: migrates with the other teens; alone, orbits the
  café edge.

## Under player requests

The block's native camera — a possessed character near the park
15–19 gets filmed if they're loud and ignored if they're not. Zee
can be asked what's happening and will produce the block's actual
history, in deadpan, for free, because the feed is the point.
