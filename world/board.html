How it works

Apartments: first month + deposit, references checked, no application fees beyond screening. Rooms in shares: the roommates pick the person, not the paperwork. Jobs: show up for the trial shift — everyone on this board hires by watching you work, not by reading a résumé. Wages posted are what they actually pay; tips and overtime vary. Listings come down when they're filled — pin yours up at Mudhaus.
All addresses on this board are within the Dolores Park neighborhood. In-game dollars; deed fees where listed are paid separately at the registry office.
