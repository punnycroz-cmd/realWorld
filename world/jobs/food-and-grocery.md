# Food & grocery workplaces — combined card

Five venues, one file: Buy-Rite Market, Buy-Rite Creamery, Dolores Perk,
Baguette About It Bakery, Il Delfino. None is a main-cast employer, so
these are the primary **job-board workplaces** for hired characters.

---

## Buy-Rite Market — 18th St grocer

**Open role:** grocery clerk, $19/h, 32 h/wk. (Parody of Bi-Rite; the
in-fiction store is scruffier and cheaper than the name suggests.)

- **The place:** full-service neighborhood grocery — produce up front,
  butcher counter in back, a clipboard schedule in the office that hasn't
  been digital since 2009. Known for stocking what the block actually
  cooks: dried chiles, masa, the good crema.
- **Shift shape:** delivery mornings Mon/Wed/Fri 6:00 (heavy, fast);
  weekend afternoons are park-crowd spillover; evenings are regulars
  buying dinner components one at a time.
- **Culture:** union-adjacent seniority culture without the union — the
  long-timers get the good shifts and everyone knows the order. Clerks
  know regulars' weekly baskets; C1 Marisol does the café's milk run here
  and is treated as trade, not customer.
- **Hiring bar:** can you lift a case, can you count, will you stay a
  year. Turnover is the enemy; a hire who lasts six months gets the good
  shifts.
- **Texture:** the PA plays the owner's salsa station; the clipboard is
  law; the butcher counter closes at 18:00 sharp and argues about it daily.

## Buy-Rite Creamery — sister window

**Open role:** scooper, $17/h, 24 h/wk.

- **The place:** walk-up window next to the market, perpetual line in
  sun, park spillover crowd. Two flavors rotate weekly; the staff picks
  and the staff are weirdly proud of their picks.
- **Shift shape:** nearly flat 12:00–21:00 in summer; fog days halve it.
  Rain kills the line entirely — scoops become cleaning shifts.
- **Culture:** youngest staff on the board, highest burnout, best
  stories. The line is a spectator scene all by itself.
- **Hiring bar:** availability on sunny weekends — the whole job is
  sunny weekends.

## Dolores Perk — park-edge café

**Open role:** counter, $18/h, 30 h/wk.

- **The place:** park-facing café tables; Esther (A05) and Ray (A19) are
  furniture. Slower rhythm than Mudhaus, tourist-tinged on weekends.
- **Shift shape:** dog-walker morning wave 8:00–10:00; park crowd all
  afternoon; rain empties it to the regulars and a paperback behind the
  counter.
- **Culture:** gentle. The counter person learns the regulars' dogs'
  names before the regulars'.
- **Hiring bar:** morning reliability and tolerance for leisure-pace
  customers. Nobody here is in a hurry; a hire who can't downshift won't
  last.

## Baguette About It Bakery — 18th/Guerrero side

**Open role:** counter, afternoons, $18/h, 25 h/wk.
(A16 Hana holds the 4:00–13:00 bake shift at $26/h — cast job, not open.)

- **The place:** production bakery with a small retail face. The block
  smells like it from 4:30. Wholesale side supplies Mudhaus's pastry case
  (the four-minutes-late driver leaves from here).
- **Shift shape:** retail face opens 7:00; the afternoon counter shift
  (the open role) is 13:00–19:00 — selling down the day's bake, marking
  half-price at 18:30, donating the rest to the church shelter run.
- **Culture:** split house — bakers' pre-dawn world vs. counter's
  afternoon world; they overlap one hour and trade notes in the doorway.
  Hana leaves a "staff loaf" marked for the counter crew.
- **Hiring bar:** afternoon availability, no fragrance on shift (rule,
  seriously — it kills the bread smell), arithmetic for the half-price
  rush.

## Il Delfino — 18th St California-Italian

**Open roles:** line cook $27/h 35 h; two servers $18/h + tips, 30 h.
(Parody of Delfina; in-fiction it's a loud, well-run neighborhood room.)

- **The place:** fifty seats, open kitchen, reservation book that still
  lives on paper. Date-night venue for the neighborhood — Priya and
  Marcus's anniversary dinner would be here, not somewhere fancy.
- **Shift shape:** dinner only, 17:00–23:00 service; prep from 14:00.
  Friday/Saturday are the real shifts; Tuesday is where new servers learn.
- **Culture:** professional kitchen energy — hot, fast, exact — softened
  by a floor crew that's been together for years. Family meal 16:15, eaten
  in twelve minutes.
- **Hiring bar:** line cook needs real sauté time somewhere; servers are
  screened on the Tuesday test — can you carry a slow night without
  checking your phone where tables can see.
- **Texture:** the paper reservation book means overbooking is a real,
  recurring, human error. The kitchen printer screaming is the room's
  stress signal.
