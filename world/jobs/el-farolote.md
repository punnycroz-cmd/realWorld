# Taqueria El Farolote — workplace card

**Kind:** late-night taqueria, Mission at 24th · **Interior:** yes
(anchor venue, request-targetable). **Owner:** the Ocampo family
(off-screen; Doña Ocampo drops in Thursdays and counts nothing but notices
everything). **Lead cook:** C8 Tomás.

## The room

Counter service, salsa bar, twenty seats, tile that has survived three
decades. Order wheel over the pass; ticket rail; the grill line is two
stations — plancha and al pastor trompo. Loud by design: corridos or the
game, always one or the other, decided by whoever opens.

## Shift shape

- **Prep 9:00–11:00:** Tomás + line cook. Beans from yesterday, salsas
  fresh, trompo stacked. This is the hour the real talk happens.
- **Lunch 11:30–14:00:** workers' crowd — Gus's crew, Cole's site crew,
  courier stop-ins. Speed over ceremony.
- **Dead 15:00–17:00:** prep top-up, staff meal at the counter.
- **Dinner → late 18:00–02:00:** families early; post-bar rush from ~23:30
  when the 600 Club lets out. The drunk-order tempo is its own discipline:
  keep it simple, keep it kind, keep them seated.

## Pay & conditions

Line cook $26/h, counter $18/h. Staff meal every shift — non-negotiable
house rule, eat standing if you must. Possessed-play bonus: a clean
post-bar rush (no comps, no walkouts) can hit +15%. Cash tips at the
counter jar, split nightly.

## Hiring bar

Line cook: Doña Ocampo watches one service, silent. She is screening for
knife safety and whether you taste the salsa before it goes out — she does
not care about résumés. Counter/closer: screens for Spanish (real, not
Duolingo) and sobriety-at-2am patience with the drunk crowd.

## Playing this workplace

- The late window is the venue's drama surface: post-bar crowds bring the
  neighborhood's loose energy in. Staff absorb it; nobody escalates; Tomás
  comping a weeping stranger's taco is plausible texture, not a rule.
- Thursday is Doña Ocampo day — the kitchen runs ten percent tighter and
  everyone knows why.
- Heat texture: the line is genuinely hot; summer evenings staff rotate
  to the salsa bar for air.
- A player-hired cook's first weeks: plancha station ownership, learning
  the ticket rail order, earning a nickname from the crew — and never
  touching Tomás's knife roll.
