# Mudhaus Coffee — workplace card

**Kind:** café, 24th St side · **Interior:** yes (anchor venue, request-targetable)
**Owner:** absentee (off-screen LLC; sends a checks-and-standards email twice
a quarter that Marisol answers in eleven words). **Manager:** C1 Marisol.

## The room

Narrow and deep. Bar along the left wall, eight two-tops, a corner armchair
claimed by laptops from 9:00 on, back door to a shared alley where staff
actually take breaks. Chalkboard menu is **Dani's** territory — she reworks
it Sundays and regulars notice. The espresso machine (a rebuilt three-group)
has a temperament: group two runs hot, and everyone except new hires knows
to pull the last shot of a batch on group one.

## Shift shape

- **Open (6:00):** Marisol or whoever's on keys. Pastry delivery ~6:20 from
  Baguette About It — it is *always* four minutes late and the driver
  always says "rain," even in September.
- **Rush 7:30–9:30:** the daily crowd scene. Line to the door; batch-mode
  behavior from the whole crew; Nadia's corner table fills by 8:45.
- **Mid-day:** two-person floor, laptops and strollers. Milk delivery Tue/Fri.
- **After-school 15:00–16:30:** A04 June's students' parents + the
  Mission High release wave — hot chocolate and cookie money.
- **Close (19:00):** Dani usually. Till count, machine backflush, alley door.

## Pay & conditions

Barista $19/h, 30–38 h offered. Shift bonus on possessed play: speed-ticket
accuracy through a rush can hit +15% (plan cap respected). Tips pooled per
shift, split by hours — honest split, Marisol enforces it. No benefits;
schedules post two weeks out and Marisol actually honors time-off requests
made before posting.

## Hiring bar (for the open barista slot)

Marisol screens: can you pull a shot without a lecture, will you show up at
6:00 for a month straight, do you bus a table without being asked. She will
train the machine; she will not train reliability. Trial shift is a real
Saturday rush — she watches hands, not talk.

## Playing this workplace

- Tempo is the story: dead 10:00 Tuesday vs. wall-to-wall Saturday are two
  different rooms. Staff chattiness tracks the line, not the clock.
- Failure texture: group two overheats mid-rush; the pastry order runs
  short on festival weekends; the Wi-Fi dies under laptop load and the
  corner table glares.
- The café is the neighborhood's rumor exchange — staff *overhear*
  everything and professionally pretend not to. Reyes' water-cup move
  (a01 card) is the house style: kindness delivered silently.
- A player-hired barista's first weeks: the lowest-status closing shifts,
  learning group two's temper, being "the new one" to regulars until the
  chalkboard or a remembered order earns a name.
