# Public sector & independent work — combined card

SF General, Mission Branch Library, the construction site, Nimbus9 remote,
dog-walking, busking, Carmen's hemming. Mixed file: institutions that pay
real wages + the informal economy that fills the gaps.

---

## SF General — hospital (public institution, real name kept)

**Cast jobs:** C4 Priya RN med-surg $48/h 36 h (3×12); A09 Asha RN same ward.
**Open roles:** two CNA/tech slots, $22/h, 36 h.

- **The ward:** med-surg floor — twelve-hour shifts, three on. The work
  is real nursing: med passes, discharges, admissions, the charting that
  never ends. Priya and Asha are colleagues first; whatever else is true
  stays off the floor.
- **Shift shape:** 07:00–19:30 days; handoff report is sacred — the
  half-hour where the ward's whole truth transfers. Night differential
  exists but the cast works days.
- **Culture:** gallows humor at the station, tenderness at the bedside,
  hierarchy that's real but human — a good charge nurse is everything.
  The cafeteria at 04:00 (for night floaters and Asha's occasional
  night pick-ups) is the staff's true break room.
- **Hiring bar:** CNA cert, lives fingerprinted, and the interview question
  that matters: "tell me about a shift that went badly." Screening for
  honesty about hard work, not polish.
- **Texture:** shift-change doubles the neighborhood's scrubs population
  at 19:30; nurses decompress at Mudhaus still in badge clips — that's
  how the block knows the hospital's mood.

## Mission Branch Library (public institution, real name kept)

**Cast job:** A11 Vera, librarian, $31/h, 40 h.
**Open role:** none (pages are union slots; a hire would be a storyline).

- **The place:** real civic texture — storytime Tuesdays, the after-school
  table 15:00–17:00 (the release wave's other landing), reference desk
  that is really a life-admin desk: forms, resumes, benefit applications.
- **Culture:** quiet, stubborn, kind. Vera's version of librarian is a
  border guard for the free world — she will find you the form, the book,
  the phone number.
- **Texture:** the library is where the neighborhood does paperwork on
  itself — job applications, lease disputes researched, citizenship
  classes. It sees the economy's underside first.

## Construction site (near Auerbach Hardware)

**Cast job:** A17 Cole, laborer, $36/h, 40 h.

- **The site:** an infill residential build, mid-frame — union-adjacent
  crew, a real schedule, a real end date that keeps slipping (fictional
  project; no real address, no real developer named — the site is "the
  Folsom lot" in-world).
- **Shift shape:** 6:30 start, flaggers and deliveries; the crew's coffee
  run to Mudhaus at 6:45 is a crowd-scene beat.
- **Texture:** site noise is the block's soundtrack; the crew lunches at
  El Farolote; weather stops work and the whole neighborhood notices the
  quiet.

## Nimbus9 — remote tech (A13 Nadia)

**Cast job:** Nadia, $50/h equivalent salary, remote from Mudhaus's
corner table. **Open role:** not a job-board posting — remote tech jobs
don't live on the block's board; that's the point.

- **The texture:** Nadia's workday is a spectator-visible mystery —
  laptop, headphones, two refills, calendar blocks as posture changes.
  Her coworkers are voices. The block knows she makes more than the shop
  owners she sits next to, and nobody says so.
- **Rules for play:** Nimbus9 stays faceless — no office venue, no
  in-world coworkers visit. If a storyline needs her job to matter, it
  surfaces through Nadia (deadline weeks, a layoff scare), never through
  the company doing anything on camera.

## Independent / informal work

- **Dog-walking (A02 Doro, $20/h equiv, 30 h):** the morning pack —
  three to five dogs, park laps 8:00–11:00. Clients are blocks
  apartments' dogs; payment is Venmo and trust. Rain splits the pack.
- **Busking (A08 Sam, ~$1,800/mo):** park corners weekend afternoons,
  the 600 Club's open-mic orbit nights. Pitch etiquette: the corner by
  the playground is mornings-claimed; Dolores & 19th is contested but
  Sam holds it by seniority.
- **Hemming & alterations (C6 Carmen, ~$300/mo cash):** word-of-mouth
  seamstress work out of 9418 Guerrero Unit A — the neighborhood's
  hems, bridesmaid fixes, mending. Payment in cash, cookies, and owed
  favors. It supplements her pension; it is also how she knows
  everyone's bodies' stories — she keeps them all.
