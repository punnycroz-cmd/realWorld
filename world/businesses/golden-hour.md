# Golden Hour Laundromat — street venue

**Code keys:** (none — minted from the reserved list, `world/businesses.md`
§2) · **Corner:** Mission St side, orange awning · **Hours:** daily
7:00–23:00 · **Interior:** no (front windows + doorway camera read)
· **Card type:** venue, tier `street`

A twelve-machine laundromat with a folding table down the middle, two
vending machines, and a bulletin board that never gets cleared. The name
is the paint job — the west window catches the late sun and the whole
room goes amber around 18:30. Whoever named it was right about one
thing: it is the warmest-lit room on the block after dinner.

It is also the neighborhood's cheapest collision venue. Nobody goes to a
laundromat for less than ninety minutes, and you can't leave — so people
sit, fold, and end up adjacent to whoever else ran out of clean clothes
the same week.

## Rhythm

- **7:00–9:30** — the early crew: service workers doing a week's wash
  before shift. Esther does hers Thursday mornings, same two machines
  by the window; she brings her own basket and her own podcast.
- **12:00–17:00** — dead quiet except the dryer's hum; one student with
  a laptop treating it as a free co-working space.
- **18:00–21:30** — the prime window. Households double up; folding-table
  adjacency is where neighbors who only nod on the street actually talk.
- **Friday night** — the laundromat date is a real genre here: two
  machines, a six-pack from Malik's, and the amber window doing the
  lighting design for free.

## Prices (game $)

Wash $4.25 · dry $0.50 per 8 min · wash-and-fold $1.75/lb (drop by
10:00, back by 18:00) · vending detergent $1.50, the thing everyone
forgets once.

## Spectator hooks (conditions, never scripts)

- The folding table forces adjacency — who ends up sharing it on a
  busy evening is information a camera can read.
- The bulletin board is a second rumor node after Malik's counter:
  lost pets, sublets, tutor flyers, one persistent "guitar lessons"
  card of Sam's that keeps migrating.
- Rain packs it — nobody line-dries in a storm, so a wet week turns
  Friday evening into standing room only.

## Request surface

Not lockable, not an event venue — it's a utility. Ambient and hired
characters route through on chore loops; a hired character doing their
own wash on screen is exactly the boring-real beat this venue exists
for.

## Boundaries

Unattended most hours (a cleaner/coin-collector comes mornings, off-
camera). No staff means no nudge surface. The bulletin board is
scenery-texture, not a feed — its flyers never carry story secrets,
and no character's private business may be posted on it as content.
