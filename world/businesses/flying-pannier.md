# Flying Pannier Courier Co-op — offstage org

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** no storefront — dispatch is a locker
wall and a group chat · **Hours:** M–F 7–19, Sat–Sun 9–17 · **Tier:**
`offstage`

Worker-owned courier co-op — pannier bags, fixed gears, rival energy vs
the apps. There is no door to point a camera at: the org exists as a
locker room, a manifest sheet, and five bikes parked in the same rack.

## Rhythm

The manifest doubles as a neighborhood gossip route — Marcus's delivery
loop threads Mudhaus, the market, the lawyer's office, the pharmacy.
Where a co-op courier has to wait is where they hear things.

## Staff

C5 Marcus (courier, $18/h ~30 h variable + tips). Not hiring — co-ops
don't churn.

## Spectator hooks

- The delivery loop is a camera path: follow Marcus and you audit the
  whole block's morning.
- Rain is the hard day — the co-op takes the deliveries the apps surge-price out of.

## Request surface

Marcus is nudge-reachable on route. No venue, no events — the org is a
schedule, not a place.

## Boundaries

Labor-dignity fiction: the co-op is stubborn and decent. No union-busting
or sabotage storylines attach to it.
