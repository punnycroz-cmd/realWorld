# Taqueria El Farolote — anchor venue

**Code keys:** `Taqueria El Farolito`, `El Farolito Bar` · **Corner:**
Mission at 24th · **Hours:** M–F 10:00–1:30, Sat–Sun 10:00–2:30 ·
**Interior:** yes · **Tier:** `anchor`

Parody of the famous little lantern — farolote is the big, slightly goofy
version, and the room knows it: oversized paper lanterns, a salsa bar with
six squeeze bottles, futbol on a wall TV that's always one channel off
what the counter wants. The register has a drawer that sticks; the cooks
slap it shut without looking.

## Rhythm

- **Lunch** — construction + office mixed; Tomás runs the line like a metronome.
- **17:00–20:00** — families; the salsa bar is a kid magnet.
- **23:00–2:00** — the post-bar rush, loudest hour of the block. Bar
  crowds spill in hungry and sentimental. This is the venue that sees
  the cast at their least guarded.

## Staff & regulars

C8 Tomás (lead cook, $30/h). Line cook and counter/closer openings on the
board ($26 / $18). Regulars: Marcus after courier shifts, A08 Sam after
gigs, whoever the bars just let out.

## Prices (game $)

Super burrito $12 · taco $3.75 · agua fresca $4 · chips & salsa $3.

## Spectator hooks

- Tomás prices suppliers at Valencia Growers Market every Saturday —
  his stall walk is a ritual scene.
- The notebook for **Pupusería La Esperanza** lives under the register.
  Its existence is surface knowledge (he talks about it); its contents
  and the $6,000 handshake loan are not.
- Rain nights compress the bar-rush into 90 minutes — the counter runs
  out of change and out of patience.

## Request surface

Venue-lockable for exclusive requests (late-night buyout is plausible).
Staff nudge-reachable on shift. Not event-capable — the room is too
small; events want The 600 Club or the park.

## Boundaries

The La Esperanza notebook never opens on camera; the loan's existence is
C7/C8's business. The owner's family stays off-screen.
