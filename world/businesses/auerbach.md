# Auerbach Hardware — anchor venue

**Code key:** `U-Save Plumbing and Hardware` · **Corner:** Mission St;
9102 Mission St, Unit 2 upstairs is Victor's flat · **Hours:** M–F 8–18,
Sat–Sun 9–17 · **Interior:** yes · **Tier:** `anchor`

Third-generation family store, and it shows: pegboard walls with key hooks
numbered in grease pencil, a brass rail that's outlived three flooring
changes, a cat that isn't officially the store's but has a bed by the
radiator. Victor knows the plumbing of half the block's Victorians
personally — which traps clog, which landlords cheat.

## Rhythm

- **Mornings** — contractors: Cole and the site crew buy fastener boxes.
- **Midday** — residents with a thing in a bag: "can you match this?"
  Victor always can.
- **Tuesdays** — repair day for the two Guerrero buildings. Work orders
  stack on the counter; this is where the landlord layer touches the
  street without ever saying "landlord."
- **After close** — Victor goes up the internal stair. The shop goes dark
  but the stair light stays on till late.

## Staff & regulars

C7 Victor (proprietor; draw, ~$6–10k/mo gross, lumpy and expense-first).
One counter-clerk opening ($21/h) he keeps meaning to fill. A17 Cole is
the most regular non-employee.

## Prices (game $)

Key cut $4 · faucet washer $1.50 · half-day tool rental $18 ·
"landlord special" supply bundles — priced by the job, cash or tab.

## Spectator hooks

- Tuesday repair stack = the visible pulse of the tenancy storyline.
- The back drawer holds a handshake ledger — off the books, off-camera,
  and off-limits. That it *exists* is surface knowledge; its contents
  are Victor and Tomás's business.
- When a main character's heater dies, the parts bag on the counter is
  the tell — spectators learn the failure before the tenant's feed does.

## Request surface

Not venue-lockable (it's a working shop), not event-capable. Victor is
nudge-reachable at the counter — but nudges about tenants get a hardware
answer. One hire slot exists; Victor screens for reliability over charm.

## Boundaries

Victor's fictional building ownership is content; the landlord ROLE stays
TBD per design §9 — no card, feed string, or surface may assert he is
"the landlord." The handshake ledger never renders its contents.
