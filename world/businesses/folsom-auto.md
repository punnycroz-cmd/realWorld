# Folsom Auto & Sons — street venue

**Code key:** `F. Lofrano and Son, Inc.` · **Corner:** Folsom side ·
**Hours:** M–F 8–17:30, closed weekends · **Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

Two-bay garage, "& Sons" despite there having been exactly one son, once,
briefly. Riffs on the real garage's shape, genericized past recognition.
The radio is always a ballgame; Gus talks to engines more than people
and the engines seem to prefer it.

## Rhythm

Weekday working hours. Mornings are drop-offs and estimates; afternoons
are the radio, the lift, and Gus narrating diagnoses to himself. The
open bay door is the signage.

## Staff

A10 Gus (mechanic, $34/h). No openings — the shop runs on him and the
lift.

## Prices (game $)

Oil change $65 · brake job $380 · "it's fine, drive it" consultation
free but you'll hear about it.

## Spectator hooks

- The day the radio isn't a ballgame, something is wrong — spectators
  learn to read it.
- Whose car is on the lift tells its own small story (Marcus's bike
  leans against the door instead — no engine, still family).

## Request surface

Nudge-reachable (Gus, in his way). No locks, no events.

## Boundaries

Honest mechanic fiction: no scam storylines land on the shop. The "&
Sons" is a joke about absence, not about any real family.
