# Dolores Perk — anchor venue

**Code key:** `Dolores Park Cafe` · **Corner:** park edge, Dolores side ·
**Hours:** M–F 7–17, Sat–Sun 7–18 · **Interior:** yes · **Tier:** `anchor`

The park's living room: battered tables, a shelf of left-behind paperbacks,
windows that fog when the rush hits. Blunt-poetry name, blunt-poetry room.
The two park-side tables nearest the window are, by unwritten treaty,
Esther's and Ray's on weekday afternoons.

## Rhythm

- Morning dog-walk crowd — Doro passes with the pack and gets her cup
  handed out the side window.
- Midday — retirees, nannies, the off-shift. Slow and warm.
- Sunny weekend — forty-minute line, zero seats, everyone takes it to
  the grass. The venue effectively empties into the park.

## Staff & regulars

No cast staff; one counter opening on the board ($18/h). Regulars:
A05 Esther and A19 Ray (afternoon parliament), C5 Marcus on off-days,
half the dog-walkers.

## Prices (game $)

Coffee $3.75 · sandwich $9.50 · cookie $3.

## Spectator hooks

- Esther/Ray's standing tables are a scene anchor — an empty pair reads
  as an event.
- Weather is the dial: fog = packed and quiet; sun = line out the door
  and the room half-empty.

## Request surface

Event-capable (a park-adjacent gathering naturally overflows here), not
venue-lockable (public park edge; locking it reads wrong). No cast staff
to nudge.

## Boundaries

Retiree regulars are scenery and presence — their conversations stay
theirs. No storyline lands on the absentee owner.
