# Buy-Rite Market + Buy-Rite Creamery — street venues

**Code keys:** `Bi-Rite Market`, `Bi-Rite Creamery` · **Corner:** 18th St,
two doors · **Hours:** market daily 8–21; creamery M–F 11–22, Sat–Sun
11–23 · **Interior:** no (counter window only) · **Tier:** `street`

Parody of the gourmet ghetto anchor: same cadence, blunt spelling. The
market is beautiful and punishing — produce arranged like a museum,
prices like a dare. The creamery is its sister window with a perpetual
line that bleeds into the produce door; the two businesses share a curb
and a mood.

## Rhythm

- **Market:** the whole cast's grocery runs pass through — Carmen buys
  single potatoes and knows exactly what everything costs; Tomás shops
  like a critic; Priya shops furious after shift.
- **Creamery:** weather-locked. Sun = line around the corner and park
  spillover; rain = two people and a wet dog.

## Staff & openings

No cast staff. Board openings: grocery clerk $19/h, scooper $17/h —
the classic player-character starter jobs.

## Prices (game $)

Fancy grocery run ~$45 · single out-of-season peach $4 · scoop $5.50 ·
sundae $9.

## Spectator hooks

- Collision density is the content: who runs into whom in aisle two.
- The creamery line is the block's visible weather vane.

## Request surface

No venue locks, no events — they are thoroughfares, not rooms. Openings
hire through the board.

## Boundaries

The parody name transforms; it does not accuse. Nothing about the real
grocer (policies, owners, incidents) attaches to Buy-Rite.
