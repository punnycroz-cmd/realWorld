# Needlepointe Tattoo — street venue

**Code key:** `13 Bats Tattoo and Piercing` · **Corner:** Clarion-adjacent ·
**Hours:** daily 12–20 · **Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

Stitch pun on a tattoo shop — the name is the joke and the category is
legible, which is exactly the house style. Flash sheets in the window,
a shop cat decal, an appointments book that's mostly names Bex drew
little skulls next to.

## Rhythm

Walk-in Saturdays are the door show; weekdays are appointments. Bex's
flash sheet rotates with her mood — regulars claim they can read her
week off the wall, and they're not entirely wrong.

## Staff

A14 Bex (artist, ~$30/h commission-weighted, ~$4.5k/mo). Shop is a
collective; no openings.

## Prices (game $)

Shop minimum $80 · flash piece $120+.

## Spectator hooks

- New flash on the window = a mood read on Bex before she says a word.
- Who's in the chair on a Saturday is spectator-visible gossip — a
  main character getting inked is a whole episode's worth of forum posts.

## Request surface

Nudge-reachable staff (Bex). No locks, no events. A player character
could plausibly book a tattoo in-world; the needle itself never gets
camera time.

## Boundaries

Body art only — nothing extreme, nothing scripted onto a character who
didn't choose it in-world.
