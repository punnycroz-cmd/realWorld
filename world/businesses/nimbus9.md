# Nimbus9 — offstage org

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** remote — Nadia's kitchen table is the
office · **Hours:** M–F 9–17 · **Tier:** `offstage`

Generic cloud-tech employer, deliberately faceless. Its entire world
footprint is a laptop, a badge on a lanyard Nadia never wears, and the
calendar that decides whether she can take a walk.

## Rhythm

Standup at 9:15, focus blocks, the Slack-equivalent pings that follow
her to Mudhaus when she works there twice a week. The job is good and
the job is nowhere — that's the texture.

## Staff

A13 Nadia (tech worker, $50/h, ~$8.65k/mo — the block's top wage, which
is its own quiet storyline about the neighborhood). Not on the job
board — remote roles aren't board postings.

## Spectator hooks

- Two laptops at the Mudhaus communal table: Nadia's employer-issued
  slab and Marisol's personal machine — work vs. secret, side by side.
- Layoff weather in tech reads as Nadia's hours, not a news event.

## Request surface

None. There is nothing to lock, book, or nudge — which is the point.

## Boundaries

Faceless by design. No product names, no office, no scandals — Nimbus9
is an income and a schedule, nothing more.
