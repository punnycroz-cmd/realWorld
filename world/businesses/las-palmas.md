# Frutería Las Palmas — street venue

**Code keys:** none (world-minted venue, `world` POI) · **Corner:**
Dolores at 19th (street stand) · **Hours:** daily 8–18, weather-locked ·
**Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

Luz's family fruit stand: two folding tables, a striped awning, a scale
that gets a calibration argument weekly. Mango cups with chile are the
signature; regulars have a standing order whether or not they show up.

## Rhythm

- **Mornings** — setup is a scene: awning up, fruit pyramids built by hand.
- **After school** — kids with dollar coins, fruit cups.
- **Rain** — the stand simply isn't there. Its absence is legible: a dry
  square of sidewalk where the awning usually drips.
