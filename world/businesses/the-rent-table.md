# The Rent Table — offstage org

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** no door — a Wednesday-evening table
in the Mission Branch Library community room · **Hours:** Wed
18:30–20:30 only · **Tier:** `offstage`

The block's tenant-counseling table (parody of tenant-rights orgs). Rung
4 of the housing grievance ladder in `world/grievances.md`: where a
notice gets read, a raise gets its math checked against the calendar,
and a paper clock gets started — habitability complaints, the 21-day
deposit rule (v54), the difference between a letter and a filing.

## Rhythm

Wednesday evening in the library basement. Vera (a11) staffs the sign-in
most weeks — a public volunteer fact; her day job is two floors up.
A paralegal volunteer checks raise math. The table writes the letter
that starts the clock, explains what a formal dispute costs in time,
and will tell a tenant honestly when the raise is legal. Advice and
paper only — it negotiates for nobody.

## Staff

A11 Vera (volunteer, sign-in). Not employment — a public volunteer
commitment, same visibility as her library hours.

## Spectator hooks

- Who sits down at the table is a quiet signal — a tenant with a folder
  is a grievance at rung 4, visible before it ever reaches the feed.
- The contested raise at 9457 Guerrero Unit 3 is the kind of folder
  this table exists for — the building is named on paper, the people
  never are.

## Request surface

None — all affordances off. Recourse texture, never a request target.

## Boundaries

Files stay private — the table's discretion is its reputation. Never
prints a name, an amount, or an archetype; the feed sees only "a housing
dispute filed — <address>" if a folder becomes paper.
