# Baguette About It Bakery — street venue

**Code key:** `Tartine Bakery` · **Corner:** 18th / Guerrero side ·
**Hours:** M–F 7–15, Sat–Sun 7–16 · **Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

The pun does the work and the bread backs it up. Small shop, glass case,
one bench outside. The oven light comes on before dawn — it is the
block's first lit window, and the crowd model treats it as the 04:00
anchor of the daypart map.

## Rhythm

- **4:00–7:00** — Hana's bake shift, invisible except for the light and
  the smell; the proofing racks are the pre-dawn scene.
- **7:00–9:00** — morning line; commuters and nurses.
- **~13:00** — sellout. The flipped CLOSED sign is a standing beat: the
  shop that runs out is a character in the neighborhood story.

## Staff & openings

A16 Hana (baker, $26/h, 4–13 shift). Board opening: afternoon counter
$18/h.

## Prices (game $)

Morning bun $5 · croissant $4.75 · country loaf $11.

## Spectator hooks

- The 4 AM lit window is how spectators know the block is alive.
- Whoever buys the last morning bun gets a visible cheer from the
  regulars in line.

## Request surface

No locks, no events. Hana is nudge-reachable in the window between bake
and counter — barely.

## Boundaries

Sellout times vary with conditions, never scripted. No real-bakery lore
attaches.
