# Calle Justa Workers' Table — offstage org

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** no door — a Thursday-evening drop-in
clinic, folding tables and a sign-in sheet · **Hours:** Thu 18:00–20:00
only · **Tier:** `offstage`

The block's worker clinic (parody of Mission worker-center culture — the
name riffs the closed real-world org *Causa Justa*). Rung 4 of the work
grievance ladder in `world/grievances.md`: where a complaint goes when
the workplace has no ear worth the name — apps, cash jobs, solo gigs —
or when the aside and the named ask have both failed.

## Rhythm

Thursday evening, every week, same folding tables. Volunteers — two
retired shop stewards and a rotating law student — read check stubs,
compare the hours a worker says they worked against the hours the stub
says, and write the demand letter the worker delivers *themselves*. The
table coaches the named ask so it lands as a request, not an accusation.
Most folders end in advice, not a filing — a clear-eyed next step is the
real product.

## Staff

Nobody is paid. Volunteers only — no cast member staffs it; it is
background civic plumbing.

## Spectator hooks

- The Thursday queue is a cross-section of the block's labor — who is
  there, and how often, is legible texture.
- A worker who signs their own letter is the table's visible success;
  a worker who won't is its honest limit.

## Request surface

None — all affordances off. An org, not a venue; never a request target.

## Boundaries

Free, bilingual, takes no cut. Will not carry a complaint the worker
won't sign their own name to. Its files never leave the table — the
discretion is the reputation. Labor-dignity fiction: the table is
stubborn and decent; no corruption storyline attaches to it.
