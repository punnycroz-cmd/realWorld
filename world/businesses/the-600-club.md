# The 600 Club — street venue

**Code key:** `500 Club` · **Corner:** Guerrero side · **Hours:** M–F
16–2, Sat–Sun 14–2 · **Interior:** no (yet) · **Tier:** `street`

Parody of the corner bar — same number-play, different door, and the
regulars will fight you about which is better. Dim, narrow, a pool table
nobody's good at, a jukebox that still takes dollar bills. The bar to be
in when it rains.

## Rhythm

- **After-work** — mechanics, couriers, the just-off-shift. Gus drinks
  one beer standing.
- **Wet Thursdays** — Sam brings his rain songs; a crowd materializes
  when the weather is bad enough.
- **Late** — Bex holds the back booth; sketchbook out, halfway between
  off-duty and still working.
- **Open-mic nights** — the venue's event surface: whoever books it
  through the bartender owns the room for two hours.

## Staff & regulars

No cast staff; the bartender is ambient crowd. Regulars: A08 Sam
(rain gigs), A14 Bex (nights), C5 Marcus (leisure), spillover from
everywhere.

## Prices (game $)

Beer $6 · well drink $9 · open-mic slot free (booked in-world).

## Spectator hooks

- Rain nights + Sam = the standing "rain songs" scene.
- Bex's booth occupancy tracks her workload at Needlepointe — a slow
  tattoo week means she's here.
- The jukebox going quiet during a conversation is a tell spectators read.

## Request surface

The block's **event venue**: open-mic bookings, a birthday, a release
party — exclusive requests can lock it for a window. This is the
canonical answer to "can I put on a show?" — yes, through the request
pipeline, rendered in-world as a booking.

## Boundaries

Bar scenes are texture: no fights-on-demand, no scripted hookups. What
happens at the booth stays the characters' call.
