# Dandy Lion Chocolate Co. — street venue

**Code key:** `Dandelion Chocolate` · **Corner:** Valencia side ·
**Hours:** M–F 10–19, Sat–Sun 10–20 · **Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

Single-origin chocolatier, pun name, serious beans. Small-batch bars
behind glass like jewelry; the smell reaches half the block. The kind of
place where a $12 bar is a decision, not a purchase.

## Rhythm

Slow all day, small spikes at lunch and after dinner — the treat stop,
not the errand. Marcus comes here on off-days to look more than buy;
the standing beat is the want he doesn't spend.

## Staff & regulars

No cast staff. Regulars: C5 Marcus (leisure), anyone buying an apology
or a thank-you — the bag itself is legible.

## Prices (game $)

Single-origin bar $12 · hot chocolate $6 · tasting square free (one,
with purchase).

## Spectator hooks

- A Dandy Lion bag in a character's hand signals intent — who receives
  it is the story.
- Rain kills the foot traffic; the counter clerk reads by the window.

## Request surface

None — it is a texture stop. (A gift arrives in-world; there's no
"send chocolate" button.)

## Boundaries

Small pleasures, honestly priced in game dollars. No real-chocolatier
practices attach to the parody.
