# Malik's Mini Mart — street venue

**Code keys:** `Guerrero Market & Deli`, `Guerrero Market` · **Corner:**
Guerrero corner · **Hours:** M–F 7–23, Sat–Sun 8–24 · **Interior:** no ·
**Tier:** `street`

Corner store, owner behind the counter twelve hours a day. Aisles you
have to sidle through, a cat on the bread rack, a bulletin board of
actual neighborhood life — room shares, lost cats, a 2009 band flyer
nobody's had the heart to take down.

## Rhythm

Steady all day; peaks at commute ends. Malik knows everyone's
cigarettes-and-milk order and asks after everyone's mother — the counter
is the block's most reliable rumor node. Whatever happened last night,
whoever stops in leaves informed.

## Staff

## Spectator hooks

- The bulletin board is a texture goldmine: room shares, lost pets,
  band flyers — world-state at a glance.
- Rain crowds the doorway; sheltering stragglers become a scene.

## Request surface

## Boundaries

The rumor node spreads what the rumor system produces — the counter
never invents facts, and no real-person gossip attaches. Bulletin-board
posts are world content only.
