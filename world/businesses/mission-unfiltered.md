# Mission Unfiltered — offstage media surface

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** anonymous neighborhood blog — a feed
surface, not a door · **Hours:** posts land at odd hours · **Tier:**
`offstage`

The anonymous blog covering the block: who was seen where, which awning
came down, what the landlord's guy was measuring. Its posts are a
world-level feed surface — rumor with a permalink.

## The secret

Marisol writes it. **The blog is public; the author is not.** This is
SECRET-adjacent: every briefing, card, and UI surface treats the author
as unknown. Spectators can *deduce* — posts appear after events the
author couldn't have attended, and the Mudhaus manager's table has a
laptop — but no surface ever confirms it.

## Spectator hooks

- Timing analysis: a post about a closed-room conversation means the
  author was closer than anyone thought — a standing spectator puzzle.
- Posts propagate through the rumor system like any account; the blog
  carries distortion risk (memory spec applies — it's a voice, not a
  camera).

## Request surface

None. No submissions, no tips line, no moderation surface — the blog is
a world object, not a UGC channel. Player requests that target "expose
the author" are secret-extraction and denied at screening.

## Boundaries

Hard rule: **the author is unleakable.** No feed event, card, briefing,
or UI string may resolve "Mission Unfiltered" to a person. Moderation
§11 applies to any request probing it.
