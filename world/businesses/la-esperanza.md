# Pupusería La Esperanza — reserved name (unbuilt venue)

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** Tomás's notebook · **Hours:** none ·
**Tier:** `reserved`

Tomás's dream restaurant — a pupusería named for hope. It exists only as
a notebook under the El Farolote register, a name checked against every
vacant storefront he passes, and a **$6,000 handshake loan from Victor**
that constitutes the entire capital stack.

## Why it's reserved, not spawned

If La Esperanza ever opens, it is an **earned world-event**: savings,
a real vacant unit, a lease through the registry — the whole grind on
camera. Nobody types a name and gets a restaurant. The drama-manager may
surface *opportunities* (a storefront goes vacant, a commissary slot
opens) but the open itself must be Tomás's decision and cost.

## Spectator hooks

- Tomás prices real spaces — the pause in front of a vacant storefront
  is a whole scene with no dialogue.
- The Victor loan is the quietest high-stakes object on the block:
  handshake, no paper, between a tenant-by-trade and the man who might
  be his landlord.

## Boundaries

The loan's existence is C7/C8 business — never in briefings, never
confirmable on camera. The notebook prop never renders readable text.
No player request may "open" or "fund" the restaurant — that's
mind-control of Tomás's arc; a request can only put an *opportunity*
in his path (§11 inject-as-opportunity), and he decides.
