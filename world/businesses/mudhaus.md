# Mudhaus Coffee — anchor venue

**Code keys:** `Haus Coffee`, `Cafe La Boheme` · **Corner:** 24th St mid-block
· **Hours:** M–F 6:30–19:00, Sat–Sun 7:00–20:00 · **Interior:** yes
(`INTERIOR_NAMES`) · **Card type:** venue, tier `anchor`

The cast's nerve center. A long narrow room: espresso bar along the left
wall, eight two-tops, a communal table at the back under a chalkboard menu
that hasn't been rewritten in two years. Brick wall, bad art rotation
(rotates because nobody claims it), one outlet everyone fights over. The
manager's table is the corner one by the hallway — it is *not* marked,
but regulars know not to sit there after 17:00.

## Rhythm

- **6:30–7:30** — contractors and nurses; drip, no talking. Cole buys two.
- **7:30–9:30** — the rush. Jules and Dani on bar, Reyes floats. This is
  the daily crowd scene; a camera parked here reads the whole cast's mood.
- **10:00–15:00** — laptops. Nadia works remote here twice a week.
- **17:00–close** — Marisol's manager hours: inventory, schedule grid,
  and (secret) Mission Unfiltered drafts on the house laptop.

## Staff & regulars

C1 Marisol (manager, $30/h), C2 Jules + C3 Dani + A01 Reyes (baristas,
$19/h). One barista opening on the board. Regulars: nearly everyone —
this is the default collision venue. Carmen nurses one drip for two hours
on Tuesdays; nobody hassles her.

## Prices (game $)

Drip $3.50 · latte $5.50 · pour-over $6.50 · day-old pastry $2 ·
"the regular" (drip + toast, unlisted) $4 — regulars only, at Marisol's
discretion.

## Spectator hooks (conditions, never scripts)

- Morning rush packs the counter — who queues together is information.
- Rain drives the park crowd inside; the room reads packed by 10:00.
- After close, the blog laptop on the manager's table is visible through
  the glass if a camera is tight — spectators can see *that* she writes;
  what she writes is the puzzle.

## Request surface

Venue-lockable (exclusive requests), event-capable (open-mic-ish evenings
are plausible; bookings would go through Marisol in-world). Staff are
nudge-reachable on shift. Job openings flow through the board, not charm.

## Boundaries

The Mission Unfiltered laptop is scenery, not a leak channel — nothing
on it resolves to text, and no surface ever confirms the author. The
absentee owner stays off-screen; no storyline lands on them.
