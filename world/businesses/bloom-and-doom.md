# Bloom & Doom Flowers — street venue

**Code key:** `Diosa Blooms` · **Corner:** 24th side · **Hours:** M–F
9–18, Sat–Sun 10–16 · **Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

Weddings and funerals both — the name is the joke, and Ida leans into
it with a window display that splits bridal white on the left and
something darker on the right. Bucket clusters out front, ribbon spools,
a cat that sleeps in the tissue paper.

## Rhythm

Steady weekdays; Saturday mornings are delivery chaos. The order book —
who ordered what, for whom, delivered where — is quietly the most
spoiler-dense object on the block, and it never opens on camera.

## Staff

A18 Ida (florist-proprietor, $25/h effective). No openings.

## Prices (game $)

Single stem $4 · bouquet $35 · wedding/funeral work quoted, never listed.

## Spectator hooks

- A big order loading into the van tells spectators *something* is
  coming — the what stays in the world.
- Valentine's week and funeral weeks have visibly different window
  displays; the shop is a mood ring for the whole cast.

## Request surface

Nudge-reachable (Ida). No locks, no events. Flowers ordered in-world
arrive in-world — there's no send-a-bouquet button.

## Boundaries

The order book is a secrets-adjacent prop: its existence is surface
knowledge, its contents are not. No storyline lands on real florists.
