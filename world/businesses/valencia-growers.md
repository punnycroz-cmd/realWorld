# Valencia Growers Market — street venue

**Code key:** `Valencia Farmers Market` · **Corner:** Valencia, weekly
stall set · **Hours:** Saturdays 8–14 only · **Interior:** no ·
**Tier:** `street`

The weekly produce market — white tents, chalkboard prices, cash
everywhere. A venue that exists one morning a week and so concentrates
its encounters: everyone who cooks shows up in the same six hours.

## Rhythm

- **8:00–10:00** — the cooks: Tomás walking stalls, comparing tomato
  cases, arguing prices with the knowledge of a man who buys for a
  restaurant. His stall walk is a spectator ritual.
- **10:00–14:00** — the households: Carmen with her rolling bag, Jules
  buying one perfect peach, Luz checking the competition's mango prices.

## Staff & vendors

Vendor collective; no cast employees. Luz's Frutería Las Palmas is the
year-round counterfactual — her regulars migrate here on Saturdays and
she notices.

## Prices (game $)

A produce bag ~$20 · flowers by the bunch $8.

## Spectator hooks

- Saturday compression: six hours, one street, everyone who cooks.
- Tomás's supplier math happens in public — his notebook comes out at
  the tomato stall.
- Rain halves the stalls and doubles the prices; the market reads thin.

## Request surface

Event-capable — a stall, a street-fair booth — as a scheduled world
event through the pipeline. Not lockable; it's a street.

## Boundaries

Vendors are extras/ambients; no real grower's name or farm attaches.
