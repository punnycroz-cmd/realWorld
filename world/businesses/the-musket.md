# The Musket — street venue

**Code keys:** `Beretta` (parody of the real Valencia restaurant —
code key only, never rendered) · **Corner:** Valencia at 23rd side ·
**Hours:** M–F 17:00–23:00, Sat–Sun 15:00–24:00 · **Interior:** no ·
**Card type:** venue, tier `street`

The neighborhood's other date-night room: small plates, a serious
cocktail list, dim room, louder bar. Where Il Delfino is white-
tablecloth intentions, The Musket is the "we're not calling it a
date" date — walk-in bar seats only, no reservations for the bar,
which means anyone can end up next to anyone and everyone knows it.

## Rhythm

- **17:00–19:00** — the decompression shift: Dani and Jules come
  post-rush some evenings, still smelling like espresso; the bar
  staff knows their order before they sit.
- **19:00–22:00** — the not-a-date window. Two bar seats between
  strangers is the venue's whole theory of social physics; who
  chooses this room over Il Delfino's formality is the read.
- **Weekend late** — industry crowd: cooks and bartenders from other
  rooms, including occasionally Tomás, who comes to study the room
  like it's homework for a restaurant that doesn't exist yet.

## Prices (game $)

Small plates $14 · cocktail $15 · the bartender's-choice off-menu
pour "whatever's honest" $12.

## Spectator hooks (conditions, never scripts)

- The bar's no-reservation policy is the venue's drama engine —
  seating adjacency is luck the audience can watch happen.
- Choosing The Musket over Il Delfino (or vice versa) is a public
  statement about what kind of evening someone thinks they're having.
- Tomás ordering one plate and asking the server three questions
  about it is his La Esperanza homework — the why stays internal.

## Request surface

Event-capable (buyout-adjacent evenings, a declared "packed bar"
night through the drama manager). Not venue-lockable — the walk-in
ethic is the point; locking it would break the fiction of the room.

## Boundaries

The rivalry with Il Delfino is a vibe, not a feud — no sabotage
beats, no "war." Both rooms are better for the other existing, and
the characters are allowed to know that.
