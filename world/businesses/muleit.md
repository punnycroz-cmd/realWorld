# MuleIt — offstage org

**Code keys:** none · **Presence:** an app — no door anywhere ·
**Hours:** 24/7 · **Tier:** `offstage`

The delivery app: stubborn, load-bearing, underpaid — the name does the
work. Its only physical footprint is Kofe's bike, the phone mount, and
the insulated bag with the peeling logo.

## Rhythm

Kofe's loop runs on the app's schedule, not his: pings dictate the day,
surge zones dictate the map. Always hiring because it is always losing
people — the churn is the business model.

## Staff

A06 Kofe (rider, $17/h variable ~$2.2k/mo + tips). Board: "always hiring"
is literally true — it's the churn slot for player characters.

## Spectator hooks

- Surge weather: rain means Kofe works whether he wants to or not.
- The app's version of a coworker is another rider at the same pickup
  counter — the two-riders-waiting scene at El Farolote is the whole
  workplace culture.

## Request surface

Hires (the churn slot). No venue, no nudges — you cannot nudge an app.

## Boundaries

Gig-work texture, not a satire target with a body count — no crash
storylines, no deactivation-as-plot-device. The critique is in the pay.
