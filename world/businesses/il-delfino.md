# Il Delfino — street venue

**Code key:** `Delfina` · **Corner:** 18th St · **Hours:** M–F 17:30–22:30,
Sat–Sun 17:30–23:00 · **Interior:** no · **Tier:** `street`

California-Italian dinner spot; the block's default date-night answer.
Window tables visible from the street — the spectator product is exactly
that: who is sitting across from whom.

## Rhythm

Dinner only. Early seatings are couples and visiting parents; late
seatings are industry and anniversaries. A Friday two-top at the window
is a public statement, and the neighborhood treats it that way.

## Staff & openings

No cast staff. Board openings: line cook $27/h, two server slots
$18/h + tips — mid-tier player jobs.

## Prices (game $)

Pasta $24 · table for two with wine ~$110.

## Spectator hooks

- The window table is a rumor generator — an unexpected pairing there
  propagates through Malik's counter by morning.
- Rain shrinks the wait-list; a table for one at the window on a wet
  Tuesday is its own sentence.

## Request surface

Event-capable (a celebration dinner is a natural exclusive-adjacent ask —
mediated through the drama layer, never a reservation UI). Not
venue-lockable; dining rooms don't lock.

## Boundaries

Food, prices, pairings — all fiction. The parody name is a nod, not a
review.
