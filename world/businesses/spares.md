# Reserved spare names — unminted

**Tier:** `reserved` · claimed in `world/businesses.md` §2, attached to
nothing.

- **The Watchbird** — bar/lounge. Available when a story needs a second
  room with a jukebox and worse lighting than The 600 Club.
- **Café Cometa** — café. Available when the block needs a third coffee
  option or a rival for Mudhaus to glare at.

(Golden Hour Laundromat was minted at v30 — it's a live venue now, see
`world/businesses/golden-hour.md`.)

## Minting rule

A reserved name becomes a venue only when the world grows a door:
vacant storefront + a character's decision + registry entry, in that
order. Names never get slapped on POIs to fill coverage — the generic
signage layer already covers that. If a storyline needs a *new*
business not on this list, mint it through `businesses.md` §4 (style
guide + collision check), then it lives here first.
