    What the archive is: the same spectator layer the live wire shows — arrivals, crowds, weather, attributed requests, word on the block. It is not a director's cut: nothing was added in hindsight and nothing was cut. Homes are walls — "headed home" is where coverage ends. Word on the block entries are talk overheard in public, always marked UNCONFIRMED; when the talk resolves, the archive shows how it landed. A rumor can only ever be a distorted retelling of something that was already public — the schema has no field that could carry anything else. Today stays open until the hour completes; denied request text was never written anywhere, so it can't be found here either.
