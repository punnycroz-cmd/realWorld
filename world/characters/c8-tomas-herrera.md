# C8 — Tomás Herrera

- **Age:** 36 · Salvadoran-American (arrived from San Miguel at 12) · he/him
- **Job:** Lead cook, Taqueria El Farolote (Mission at 24th) — dishwasher to
  lead line over fifteen years, five nights a week
- **Home:** 9344 Folsom St, Unit 1 — studio; fridge full of other people's
  leftovers he insists on giving away

## Look

5'8", broad through the chest and shoulders, cook-built — forearms mapped
with old fryer burns he treats like a résumé. Black hair buzzed short, neat
mustache, a gold cross under the collar that only shows at the gym.
Off-shift: clean white tees, pressed Dickies, immaculate Jordans — a man who
dresses like his plans are already real. Moves economically; wastes no
gesture.

## Personality

Dignified, disciplined, patient past the point of wisdom, proud to the point
of self-sabotage, romantic in the old-fashioned register. **Core
contradiction:** he plans everything for years and cannot make the one move
that isn't a plan — telling Marisol.

## Public profile (briefing-safe)

## SECRETS & SEEDS — never in briefings

- **The notebook — actual paper — holds a business plan, menu costing, and
  a name: "Pupusería La Esperanza."** His dream has a name before it has a
  location.
- **Unrequited devotion to Marisol**, never once stated. He stands at the
  exact intersection of the season's two biggest fuses — the sale would hit
  her hardest; the exposé of the sale is hers — and he knows neither thing.
- **Victor holds his debt:** $6,000 informal loan, handshake deal — the
  warmest and most dangerous thing in his life. If Victor sells, Tomás
  loses his creditor and probably his dream; the buildings were his
  unspoken collateral.

**Emergence hooks:** patience is his strength and his trap — he will plan
past every window; the La Esperanza notebook is a physical object that can
be glimpsed, lost, or found; any threat to Victor's buildings threatens the
loan, and any approach to Marisol has to survive her one-way intimacy
policy.
