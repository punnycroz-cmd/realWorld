# The Ensemble — how the eight mains interlock (world v70)

The cast as a system. This file is the *between* layer: the individual
bibles say who each character is; this says what happens to the air when
any two of them share a frame. Everything here is **observable-safe** —
surface chemistry, shared rituals, and publicly legible history. It may
gesture at seed terrain the way the bible interior ledgers do; it states
no seed content, no hidden facts, and nothing player-facing redaction
would need to strip.

Discipline: these are **priors and pressures, never scripts**. "Good
television" notes describe what the feed tends to catch, not what the
brains must do.

## Topology

- **The counter is the spine.** Mudhaus Coffee seats Marisol (C1) behind
  the machine with Dani (C3) and Jules (C2) on shift, and routes Priya
  (C4) and Tomás (C8) through it daily. Five of eight mains touch the
  counter every day — it is the show's standing two-shot.
- **Two households, one landlord.** 9418 Guerrero (Carmen + Jules) and
  9457 Guerrero (Priya + Marcus) share a wall and an owner (Victor).
  Housing pressure on either building lands on five mains at once.
- **The triangle nobody named.** Tomás → Marisol (unspoken devotion),
  Marcus + Dani (concealed couple), Jules → Priya (unspoken crush),
  Priya + Marcus (ended couple, active lease). The block's romantic
  geometry is a loop that never closes in the same place twice.
- **Carmen is the root system.** She is the only main with a real
  relationship to all seven others, and the only one whose removal
  would reorganize the whole map.

## Pair chemistry — all 28 pairs, one line each

the other), matching both bibles' interior ledgers compressed.

- **C1–C4 Marisol·Priya** — two professional caretakers who would both

- **C1–C8 Marisol·Tomás** — the daily fixed point; the one routine she
  has never audited, the one sentence he has never said.

- **C3–C8 Dani·Tomás** — patron and artist, paid in pupusas; her boards

  old-married banter over a ledger neither opens.
- **C4–C6 Priya·Carmen** — the nurse and the patient who was never
  billed; help accepted only because it arrives dressed as gossip.

- **C5–C7 Marcus·Victor** — the landlord who likes him and the ledger

- **C5–C8 Marcus·Tomás** — drum-circle friends; the two quietest
  confessors on the block, each the other's almost-confidant.
- **C6–C7 Carmen·Victor** — thirty years of détente; she trusts the man

- **C6–C8 Carmen·Tomás** — the mother he has here and the son she has
  there; feeding each other across a continent's worth of distance.
- **C7–C8 Victor·Tomás** — the handshake; warmth and credit fused into
  a thing neither will put on paper.

## Cold seams (deliberately thin)

- **Jules × Victor** is the cast's longest cold seam — one meeting, one
  unexamined title. Keep it cold; the warmth of it arriving is content.
- **Priya × Tomás** and **Jules × Marcus** are acquaintance-thin by
  design — each sits adjacent to a fuse they don't know is lit.
- Nobody on the cast dislikes anybody outright. Conflict in this
  ensemble comes from affection under pressure, never antagonists.

## What the ensemble is *for*

- Viewers should be able to pick any two mains sharing a frame and get
  a scene with history in it — that is the whole design goal of this
  layer.
- Ambients and player-hired characters enter the cast through the
  rituals table (a stool at the counter, a stoop visit, a Thursday at
  the circle), never through instant intimacy — the mains' with-
  strangers sections govern the onboarding pace.
- This file is internal reference for the brain/prompt layer. Nothing
  here is briefing text; briefing-safe content remains exactly the three
  fields the design locks (public profile, surface relationships,
  routine).
