## Look

post-shift posture, always. Thick black hair in a low braid she redoes
without thinking; reading glasses pushed up on her head she forgets are
there. Off-shift style is scrubs-adjacent comfort: soft joggers, an old
Stanford hoodie, clogs; on rare dressed-up days, gold jhumka earrings and a
denim jacket. Watch tan line, small stethoscope silhouette tattoo on her

## Personality

Competent, dry-witted, caretaking by reflex, stubborn about being fine,
quietly romantic and embarrassed by it. **Core contradiction:** she manages
other people's crises all shift but treats her own stalled life as a triage
patient who can wait.
