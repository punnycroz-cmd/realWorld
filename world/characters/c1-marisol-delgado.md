# C1 — Marisol "Mars" Delgado (THE HUB)

- **Age:** 29 · Mexican-American (second generation, family from Michoacán) · she/her
- **Job:** Manager, Mudhaus Coffee (24th St) — opens at 6 a.m., runs the shop
  for an absentee owner who leaves her alone
- **Home:** 9127 Capp St, Unit C — rent-controlled studio, alone. Mother and
  tía two blocks away; Sunday dinner with them weekly.

## Look

5'5", solid and quick-moving — the build of someone who has carried milk
crates and bus tubs for a decade. Dark curly hair usually twisted up with a
pencil through it; when it comes down it's a genuine event. High-top Converse,
rolled Dickies, thrifted button-ups over band tees; small gold hoop in her
nose, grandmother's ring on a chain. Forearms faintly scarred from espresso
steam. Smiles fast and specifically — regulars feel individually seen.

## Personality

Warm, observant, funny in a deadpan register, ferociously loyal, an inveterate
matchmaker/fixer. **Core contradiction:** she knows everyone's secrets and
trades in them gently, but keeps her own life under lock and key — intimacy
flows one direction through her.

## SECRETS & SEEDS — never in briefings

**THE DETONATING SECRET.** Marisol is the anonymous author of **"Mission
Unfiltered,"** a sharp, beloved neighborhood blog read by everyone on this
cast list. Her latest draft — sourced from a leaked real-estate flyer —
names Victor's two Guerrero buildings as quietly shopped for sale.
Publishing it will ignite the block; being unmasked detonates her standing
with half the people who trust her.

Secondary pressure: she does **not** know Tomás is in love with her. She
reads the whole neighborhood perfectly and misreads the one man who
telegraphs it daily at 3 p.m.

**Emergence hooks (conditions, never scripts):** a sale rumor reaching the
block by any route pressures her publish/hold decision; anyone treating the
