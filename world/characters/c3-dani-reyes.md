# C3 — Dani Reyes

- **Age:** 24 · Filipino-American (family in Daly City) · she/her
- **Job:** Barista, Mudhaus Coffee — and the café's unofficial visual
  identity (chalkboards, window art, cup-sleeve doodles regulars collect)
- **Home:** 9263 Geneva Ave, Unit 4 — crowded Outer Mission flat shared with
  two cousins; functionally lives on 24th St six days a week

## Look

5'3", compact and kinetic, forever leaning on the counter like it might leave
without her. Jaw-length black hair dyed defiant copper at the ends; heavy
dark bangs. Cropped work pants, oversized tees she's drawn on herself, chunky
sneakers; a constellation of stick-and-poke tattoos on her fingers and one
excellent professional koi on her shoulder. Always a pen behind one ear —
usually leaking.

## Personality

Quick, playful, sharp-tongued when nervous, genuinely talented and genuinely
broke, brave about everyone else's feelings. **Core contradiction:** draws
the neighborhood with merciless honesty but lies by omission about her own
life — the secrecy is starting to be the most interesting thing about her.

## SECRETS & SEEDS — never in briefings

**Three months of secretly dating Marcus** — Priya's ex and current roommate.
Every day it stays hidden it gets worse: Marcus wants to tell, Dani keeps
delaying, and Marisol is starting to notice the chalkboard doodles look like
someone specific. Marisol would be the person most hurt by the secret's
existence AND its concealment — she is Dani's sister figure and Dani's
boyfriend's ex's confidante at once.

The unexplained evening stops (17:00–21:00) are the visible seam: she is
somewhere she doesn't talk about — Marcus's orbit, Clarion Alley walls, both.

**Emergence hooks:** art is her tell — any viewer or character who reads the
chalkboards closely can triangulate the secret before it breaks; the
block-crisis season (building sale) compresses everyone into each other's
kitchens, which is when hidden couples get seen.
