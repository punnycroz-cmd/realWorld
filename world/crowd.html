Crowd rules under audit

  • Extras spawn/despawn off-camera only — at block edges, through doors. Nothing pops in view.
  • Extras carry nothing: no name, id, ledger row, feed attribution, memory, or request handle. Walk chains reuse a silhouette — costume continuity, not identity.
  • ≤3 interactive pawns within range of any main or player character.
  • 00–04 may be deserted. The honest hour is protected — nothing pads it.
  • Minors (June, Zee) stay public-space; greet in packs; never request targets.
  • Locked venues clear extras, not staff — a claim empties the crowd, not the people who work there.
  • Shades, flows, micro-events, seasons stay internal. The wire says band words and scene labels only — a gray morning is a reading, never the word "fog_season".
  • Fog is a depth profile, not a weather binary — it thins the west edge first and burns by ~10:30. An all-day fog sends the bench parliament to Dolores Perk; nobody runs, nobody shelters.
  • The calendar owns its days. An annual shade (Carnaval, muertos, a home game) suppresses the day shades, never a routine — Ida still opens Bloom & Doom; the line just forms.
  • Idle has an honest name. Postures are dwell conditions on zone kind and pair availability — nobody is dispatched into one, and no label may imply sleep. Minors pair only within the pack.
  • Extras arrive with company. Lone/duo/cluster units share one spawn and one despawn; budgets still count bodies. Kid-scale silhouettes live only inside family clusters — never lone, never bars, never overnight.
  • Courtesies are conditions. A beat renders only while its ambient's state, an extra's presence, and the cooldown all hold — gesture only, no dialogue, no ledger. Minors appear in no beat.
  • The bench answers loans. A request can borrow an ambient's presence (in-role, in-bounds, ≤90 min); the post reads understudy / sign / open while they're gone — never a lie, never a feed word. Minors are never pullable.
