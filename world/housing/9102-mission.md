# 9102 Mission St — mixed-use storefront + flat

**Owner (fiction):** Victor Auerbach (the building, the store, and the
flat — owner-occupied). **Registry:** `bld-m9102`, Unit 2 (1BR),
`status: owner-occupied`, rent $0.

## The building (SURFACE)

The hardware store's upstairs flat — the classic Mission arrangement:
shop below, home above, one staircase connecting a man's work and his
life. The flat has the proportions of a hundred-year-old commercial
building: tall windows on Mission St, streetcar-wire hum in the glass,
floor that creaks in a pattern the store's regulars could set a watch to
if the store were open at night.

- **Unit 2 (1BR — Victor):** spare and orderly — a widower's flat kept
  at the standard of a man who fixes things: nothing broken stays broken
  here. The kitchen table is where the repair ledger gets reconciled
  Sunday nights. The window over the awning is his observation post —
  he sees Mission St's whole day from it without appearing to watch.
- The back stair connects the flat to the store's stock room — Victor
  is at the counter ninety seconds after his alarm.

## Quirks

- Living over your own store: delivery trucks at 7:00, the security
  grate's rattle at close, the store's heat rising through the floor in
  winter (the one free amenity).
- The awning window makes Victor the block's informal watcher — people
  who need him don't knock; they look up.
- The building is self-maintained: the only address in the file where
  the landlord and the tenant are the same man and the repair list gets
  done the same week.
