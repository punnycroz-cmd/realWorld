# In-world listings — player-facing copy (world v3)

How housing appears **inside the world** — the board at Mudhaus, the
listings page a hired character scrolls on the apartment hunt (v6 flow).
Written like real SF listings: terse, photo-implying, slightly euphemistic.
All addresses fictional 9xxx (address spec); rents are game dollars.

House style for all listing copy:
- Lead with the truth that sells (light, rent-controlled, near the park).
- One honest flaw, lightly worded ("cozy," "lively stair," "city sounds").
- Landlord voice varies by building — Victor's listings are shorter and
  gruffer; the landlord-role listings are neutral-managerial.
- Never list a real SF business as a landmark — parody names only
  ("two blocks from Mudhaus Coffee," not real cafés).
- No rent bids, no application fees above screening cost — honest-sim
  rules, no dark patterns.

## Application texture (what renting costs beyond rent)

- **Move-in costs:** first month + deposit (1× for flats, 0.5× for rooms).
  No application fee scams — screening cost only, and listings say so.
- **Screening conditions (not scripts):** references for Victor's
  buildings (he calls them, actually talks); income ~2.5× rent for the
  landlord-role listings; roommates interview for shares.
- **Timing:** listings post when a unit turns over (registry event);
  the listed vacancies above are the live board as of v3.
- **Purchase path:** rent → buy within the same address system; deed fee
  in credits per monetization §2.4 (PROPOSAL numbers, unchanged).
