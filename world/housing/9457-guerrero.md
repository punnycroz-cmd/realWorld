# 9457 Guerrero St — Victorian three-flat

**Owner (fiction):** Victor Auerbach. **Registry:** `bld-g9457`, Units 1–3,
all rent-controlled.

## The building (SURFACE)

The taller sibling of 9418 — three stacked flats, the pink Victorian the
block actually calls "the pink building." Shared stair with a runner
that's older than every tenant. Unit 3 is the upper flat with the bay
window and the view that makes the rent.

- **Unit 1 (1BR, $1,950/mo):** an ambient household — quiet neighbors
  whose names are on the mailbox and nowhere else. They work, they get
  packages, they say hi on the stair.
- **Unit 2 (studio, LISTED $1,350/mo):** middle flat, small, backs onto
  the stair landing — you hear the building's comings and goings. The
  trade for the cheapest legal rent in the file. Listing copy in
  `listings.md`.
- **Unit 3 (2BR upper flat, $3,200/mo — Priya + Marcus):** bay window,
  real kitchen, the flat two incomes can almost afford. The dead heater
  is the flat's known thing: registered with the landlord, unrepaired,
  disputed alongside the raise. Marcus's bike lives in the hall — the
  building tolerates it because everyone tolerates it.

## Quirks

- The stair carries sound vertically — Unit 3 hears Unit 2's door, Unit
  1 hears everyone. New tenants learn to walk soft by week two.
- The pink paint is a landmark: directions on the block run "past the
  pink building."
- Unit 3's radiator died last winter; a space heater does the job badly
  and the electric bill shows it.
- Garbage night is Unit 1's unspoken job; other units owe them cookies.
