# 9418 Guerrero St — Victorian duplex

**Owner (fiction):** Victor Auerbach. **Registry:** `bld-g9418`, Units A–B,
both rent-controlled.

## The building (SURFACE)

A narrow two-unit Victorian, powder-blue with white trim that's been
repainted the same color since the 80s. Shared front stair; Unit A is the
lower flat (parlor level), Unit B the upper. The entry hall smells faintly
of Carmen's cooking — garlic and coffee grounds — and of the rosemary bush
she's claimed by the gate. The bell for Unit A is taped; you knock.

- **Unit A (2BR, $950/mo, rent-controlled — Carmen since 1989):** the
  building's anchor flat. Sewing machine by the window, hemming work on
  the table, the good couch with a sheet over it. The spare room is
  Jules's — an informal arrangement, cash, no paperwork; the hall knows
  Jules lives there and treats it as unremarkable Mission subletting.
- **Unit B (1BR, LISTED $2,100/mo):** upper flat — bay window on Guerrero,
  street light at night, footsteps of Unit A below mostly absorbed by
  lath-and-plaster. See `listings.md` for the listing copy.

## Quirks

- The hot water is generous but the pressure drops when both flats
  shower — residents learn each other's mornings.
- The entry-hall outlet is where everyone's phone charges during
  emergencies; it's an informal commons.
- Carmen feeds the block's cats at the side gate at 17:00; the cats are
  address fixtures regardless of tenancy.
- Victor fixes things here faster than anywhere — it's his own building
  and Carmen's been in it since before his son was born. The Tuesday
  table sees 9418 items first.
