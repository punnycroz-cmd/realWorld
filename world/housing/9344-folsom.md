## The building (SURFACE)

A low studio block on Folsom near the auto shops — the street smells like
a garage in the good way, and the construction site is two doors down
(Cole's lot), so the building wakes with the crew at 6:30 whether it wants
to or not. Ground-floor units, security gate, a shared side yard with a
clothesline and someone's tomato pots.

- **Unit 1 (studio, $1,275/mo — Tomás):** ground floor, front — he hears
  the gate every time anyone comes home, which is fine because he keeps
  cook's hours anyway. The flat is a cook's flat: good knives, a real
  cutting board, a stockpot that doesn't fit in the cupboard, a notebook
  on the shelf. He leaves for El Farolote at 10:15 and returns after
  midnight on late weeks; neighbors know him by smell of the epazote
  that travels with him.

## Quirks

- The gate slams. Everyone in Unit 1–3 knows exactly when anyone arrives.
- The clothesline yard is the commons — tomatoes in season, shared

- Construction noise 6:30–15:30 is the building's clock; Saturdays are
  blessedly quiet.
- Tomás's late-night cooking (staff meal leftovers reheated) drifts into
  the hall; the building considers this a perk.
