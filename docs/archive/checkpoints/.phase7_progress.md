## 1. Summary of Work Done in Slice 7A

### Institutional Customary Court (`src/systems/29a_court.js`)
- Implemented `CustomaryCourt` system replacing automatic punishment with formal customary hearings.
- **Hearing Architecture**:
  - `selectPresidingElder(hearing)`: selects the eldest living adult non-party villager, tie-broken by social reputation (`getVillagerReputation`).
  - `isEligibleWitness(villager, hearing)`: enforces strict **local belief** check. Only villagers who directly perceived the event via senses (proximity $\le 12$ cells and conscious) or hold an un-superseded epistemic memory are eligible. Witnesses who were far away without memory are rejected (`negative control 3d`).
  - `produceTestimony(witness, hearing, witnessRecord)`: produces belief tuples `{ claim, suspect, source, confidence, fidelity }`. Supports fidelity traits and memory distortions (`mistakenSuspect` / `mistakenWho`).
  - `holdCourtHearing(params)`: collects testimonies, weighs credibility vs defense plea, reaches verdict (`restitution`, `public_labor`, or `exile`), and records decaying reputation reasons (`COURT_VERDICT_DECAY = 0.95`).
  - **Material Conservation in Restitution**: Real transfer of identified items (`recordTransfer`) and currency between parties without void creation or magic gold.
  - **Wrongful Conviction & Emergent Retaliation Narrative (3a)**: If an innocent person is convicted based on mistaken witness testimony, the victim of injustice forms a persistent grievance reputation reason (`grievance_false_testimony`, weight $-1.0$, decay $0.98$), elevates grudges ($\ge 3$), checks rivalry, and executes downstream retaliatory actions (insult/brawl).

### Institutional Trade Guilds & Apprenticeship (`src/systems/29b_guilds.js`)
- Implemented 3 canonical trade guilds:
  - `smith`: Blacksmiths / Metal & Crafting Guild (primary skill: `building`).
  - `baker`: Bakers / Food & Cooking Guild (primary skill: `cooking`).
  - `farmer`: Farmers / Agriculture Guild (primary skill: `farming`).
- Roster & membership management with ranks (`apprentice`, `journeyman`, `master`).
- **Apprenticeship Skill Multiplier (3b)**:
  - `getGuildApprenticeshipBonus(villager, skill)`: Apprentices training under a designated guild master receive a deterministic $1.60\times$ multiplier on XP gains in `gainXP` (`src/systems/14d_skills.js`).
  - Strict negative control: Outsiders and self-taught practitioners receive exactly $1.0\times$ (zero bonus).
- **Caravan Bloc Pricing (3c)**:
  - `getCaravanGuildSellPrice(what, seller, qty, baseBuyPrice, famBonus)`: Guild members selling bulk lots ($\ge 10$ units, e.g. 10 bread) to traveling caravans receive a bulk contract unit price higher than retail piecemeal sales ($5\text{g/unit} > 4\text{g/unit}$).
  - Strict negative control: Non-guild sellers receive standard retail unit price ($4\text{g/unit}$).
  - Conservation of matter: 10 units deducted from seller, 10 units added to caravan stock, gold transferred accurately.

## 3. Mathematical Formulas & Invariants

### Customary Court Deliberation:
$$\text{credibility}_i = (\text{source}_i == \text{'direct'} ? 1.0 : 0.6) \times \text{conf}_i \times \text{fidelity}_i \times (0.5 + 0.5 \times \text{rep}_i)$$
$$\text{prosecutionWeight} = \sum_{i \in \text{witnesses, accused==defendant}} \text{credibility}_i$$
$$\text{convictionThreshold} = (\text{plea} == \text{'admission'} ? 0.3 : (\text{plea} == \text{'denial'} ? 0.6 : 0.5))$$
$$\text{isGuilty} = \text{prosecutionWeight} \ge \text{convictionThreshold}$$

### Apprenticeship Skill Gain:
$$a = \text{amt} \times (\text{weak} ? 0.5 : (\text{strong} ? 1.6 : 1.0)) \times (\text{isApprenticeWithMaster} ? 1.60 : 1.0) \times \max(0.25, 1 - \text{lvl} / 12)$$

### Caravan Bloc Pricing:
$$\text{retailUnitPrice} = \max(1, \lfloor \text{baseP} \times (1 + 0.5 \times \text{disc}) \rfloor)$$
$$\text{guildBlocUnitPrice} = \max(\text{retailUnitPrice} + 1, \text{round}(\text{baseP} \times (1 + 0.5 \times \text{disc} + 0.35)))$$
