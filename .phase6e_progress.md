### D1: Three Decay Accumulators (Dt-scaled, conditional explicit)
- **Coupling 1 (`hunger↑ → stress↑`)**:
  - Accumulator: `v.hungerStressAcc`.
  - Condition: `satiety < 0.35`. Inflow `rate = ((0.35 - satiety)/0.35) * 0.25`. Eating full (`satiety >= 0.35`) sets rate to 0.
  - Decay: hourly factor `0.50`. Analytical dt-scaled integration `decayIntegrate(acc, decayRate, rate, dtH)`.
  - Effect: feeds into `b.stress` and `v.mood`.
  - Differential probe result: 6h starvation monotonically increases accumulator from 0.0 to 0.2029; eating full decays accumulator back to 0.000000 in subsequent hours.
- **Coupling 2 (`fear↑↑ → exhaustion`)**:
  - Accumulator: `v.fearFatigueAcc`.
  - Trigger: Ingesting fear signal sets `v.fearFatigueAcc = min(1.0, acc + signal.intensity)`.
  - Decay: hourly factor `0.215` ($0.215^3 \approx 0.0099 \le 0.01$, expires after ~3 hours).
  - Effect: boosts fatigue accumulation rate by `v.fearFatigueAcc * 0.15 * dtH` while active.
  - Differential probe result: fatigue rate is significantly elevated during hours 1–3 vs control pawn; at hour 3.5+ accumulator decays to 0 and fatigue accumulation rate returns to normal baseline.
- **Coupling 3 (`pain↑ → patience↓`)**:
  - Accumulator: `v.painPatienceAcc`.
  - Condition: `pain > 0.15` (wounds and injuries). Inflow `rate = (pain - 0.15) * 0.80`.
  - Decay: hourly factor `0.50`.
  - Patience getter: `patience = clamp(1.0 - v.painPatienceAcc, 0.0, 1.0)`.
  - Social utility modifier: wired into `src/brain/utility.js` `scoreCandidateAction` for `socialize_` actions. If `patience < 1.0`, penalizes score by `(1.0 - patience) * 30.0`.
  - Differential probe result: high pain pawn exhibits `patience = 0.798` vs healthy pawn `patience = 1.0`; socialize action utility score is measurably reduced by 6.06 points.
- **Tick-Size Invariant Determinism**:
  - Analytical differential integration ($\frac{da}{dt} = R - k a$) guarantees identical values regardless of tick frequency.
  - Differential probe result: running 3h with $3 \times 1.0\text{h}$ vs $6 \times 0.5\text{h}$ vs $12 \times 0.25\text{h}$ produces error $< 2.8 \times 10^{-17}$ (machine epsilon).
